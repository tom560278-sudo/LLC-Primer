The main difference is the type of business tools each platform emphasizes. Tailor Brands combines LLC formation with business-building and branding tools, particularly on its Elite plan. ZenBusiness combines formation with compliance services, business documents, support and business-management tools.

Neither platform is the right fit for every business. The more useful question is which features you actually need and whether the recurring cost of those services makes sense for your LLC.

Frequently Asked Questions

01 Which is better: Tailor Brands or ZenBusiness?
Neither is universally better. Tailor Brands places more emphasis on branding and business-building tools, particularly through its Elite plan. ZenBusiness places more emphasis on formation, compliance and ongoing business services. The appropriate choice depends on which features matter to your LLC.
02 Is ZenBusiness cheaper than Tailor Brands?
It depends on the plan and services you need. ZenBusiness currently lists Starter at $0, Pro at $199/year and Premium at $299/year, while Tailor Brands lists Lite at $0, Essential at $199/year and Elite at $249/year. The services included are different.
03 Does either include a registered agent?
Both charge $199/yr for registered-agent service separately. Neither Tailor Brands nor ZenBusiness includes registered-agent service with its formation packages.
04 Does Tailor Brands include an operating agreement?
Essential and Elite include an operating agreement. The Lite plan does not.
05 Does ZenBusiness include an operating agreement?
Pro and Premium include an operating agreement template. Starter does not include it by default.
06 Does Tailor Brands include EIN?
EIN filing is a paid add-on. Eligible businesses can also obtain an EIN directly from the IRS at no charge.
07 Does ZenBusiness include EIN?
EIN is included with Pro and Premium plans. Starter users need to add it separately.
08 Which has faster processing?
Both offer 1-day processing on their paid tiers. ZenBusiness Starter is 7–10 days; Tailor Brands Lite is 14 days. State processing is separate.
09 Are state fees included?
No. The advertised formation prices are separate from the state's LLC filing fee.
10 Can I form an LLC without using either?
Yes. You can file directly with your state's business filing office without using a formation provider.
