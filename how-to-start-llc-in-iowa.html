How to Start an LLC in Iowa in 2026

Last reviewed: September 2026  ·  Primary sources: Iowa Secretary of State, Iowa Department of Revenue

To start an LLC in Iowa in 2026, file a Certificate of Organization with the Iowa Secretary of State, appoint an Iowa registered agent, provide the required business information, and pay the $50 state filing fee. Iowa LLCs also file a Biennial Report every two years. The online report fee is currently $30, while paper filing costs $45.

Iowa uses the Secretary of State’s Fast Track Filing system for online business filings. The system can be used to form an LLC, reserve a business name, change a registered agent or office, file biennial reports, and complete other business filings. Iowa’s individual income-tax rate for 2026 is 3.8%, while Iowa’s state sales-tax rate is 6% (many cities and counties add a 1% local option tax).

Can You Form an Iowa LLC Yourself?

No formation company or lawyer is required to create an Iowa LLC. The Iowa Secretary of State provides an online Fast Track Filing system for LLC formation. A formation service can still be useful if you want help preparing the filing, registered-agent coverage, document handling, or additional business services.

Choose a Name for Your Iowa LLC

Iowa requires an LLC’s name to be distinguishable from other registered business entities. The Secretary of State provides a Business Entities Search to check existing names before filing. Your proposed name should also satisfy Iowa’s naming requirements and use an appropriate LLC designation.

Use the Iowa Secretary of State’s business search to look for existing entities with similar names. Iowa’s search system applies certain search refinements, so a simple exact-name search should not be treated as a guarantee that your proposed name will be approved. The final determination of name availability is made during the state’s filing process.

ℹ Name Reservation Note: Iowa allows an available entity name to be reserved for 120 days. The current reservation fee is $10. A name reservation is optional. Important: If you reserve a name through Fast Track Filing, the Secretary of State’s instructions state that the formation documents must then be completed on paper rather than through the online formation process.

Iowa also provides a Fictitious Name filing for applicable businesses (current fee: $5). A fictitious or assumed business name does not create a separate LLC — your existing LLC remains the legal entity responsible for the business’s obligations.

Appoint an Iowa Registered Agent

An Iowa LLC must maintain a registered agent for service of process and official business correspondence. The Iowa Secretary of State states that an individual acting as a registered agent must reside in Iowa, be at least 18 years old, and have an office within Iowa. A business entity can also serve when it satisfies the state’s requirements.

Create an Operating Agreement

Iowa does not require you to file an Operating Agreement with the Secretary of State as part of the LLC formation filing. An Operating Agreement can document how the LLC will be owned and managed, including ownership percentages, capital contributions, management responsibilities, voting rights, profit and loss allocations, member admission and withdrawal, transfer restrictions, and dissolution procedures. The Operating Agreement is an internal business document — keep it with your LLC records.

Get an EIN and Open a Business Bank Account

An EIN is a federal tax identification number issued by the IRS. The EIN itself is available free from the IRS. Do not pay a third-party website simply to obtain the federal number when you can apply directly through the IRS EIN application. See our EIN guide for details.

A separate business bank account keeps LLC finances organized. Banks commonly ask for: approved Certificate of Organization, EIN confirmation, government-issued identification, Operating Agreement, and ownership or authorization information. Requirements vary by institution. See our business bank account guide.

Iowa LLC Taxes Explained

Iowa’s LLC tax treatment depends partly on how the LLC is classified for federal and Iowa tax purposes. A single-member LLC may generally be treated as a disregarded entity for federal income-tax purposes unless it makes a different election. A multi-member LLC is generally treated as a partnership unless it elects another classification.

⚠ Iowa Sales Tax Rate Correction: Iowa does not have a universal 7% sales-tax rate everywhere. Iowa’s state sales and use tax rate is 6%. Many cities and counties also impose a 1% local option sales tax, meaning the combined rate can be 7% in an applicable jurisdiction — but not every location has the local option tax. Always check the current Iowa Department of Revenue rate information for your specific transaction location.
Iowa Pass-Through Entity Tax (PTET): Iowa allows qualifying partnerships and S corporations to elect the PTET. This is an optional tax election rather than a standard LLC formation charge. A single-member LLC treated as a disregarded entity is not eligible to make its own PTET election. Evaluate PTET and S-corp elections with a qualified Iowa tax professional. Iowa LLC Biennial Report and Ongoing Compliance

Iowa LLCs file a Biennial Report every two years rather than annually. The Secretary of State’s current schedule states that LLCs file during odd-numbered years, beginning January 1 and with the report due by April 1. The online filing fee is $30 and the paper filing fee is $45.

Iowa’s basic LLC formation cost is straightforward: the domestic Certificate of Organization costs $50, and LLCs generally file a $30 online Biennial Report every two years (due April 1 in odd-numbered years). Choose a qualifying registered agent, keep your LLC records current, and determine whether your business needs Iowa tax registrations or local licenses.

Frequently Asked Questions

1How much does it cost to start an LLC in Iowa?
The Iowa Secretary of State charges $50 for a domestic LLC Certificate of Organization. The LLC also has a $30 online Biennial Report requirement every two years ($45 when filed on paper). Optional costs such as registered-agent services, name reservations, licenses, taxes, and professional assistance are separate.
2Does Iowa have an annual report for LLCs?
No. Iowa LLCs file a Biennial Report every two years. The current schedule places LLC reports in odd-numbered years, with reports due by April 1. The online fee is $30.
3Does Iowa require a registered agent?
Yes. An Iowa LLC must maintain a registered agent for service of process and official documents. An individual serving in that role must satisfy Iowa’s residency, age (at least 18), and office requirements.
4Can I be my own registered agent in Iowa?
Yes, if you satisfy Iowa’s requirements. The Secretary of State states that an individual registered agent must reside in Iowa, be at least 18 years old, and have an office within Iowa.
5How much is an Iowa name reservation?
The current name-reservation fee is $10, and an approved reservation holds the name for 120 days. A reservation is optional. Note: if you reserve a name through Fast Track Filing, the Secretary of State’s instructions state that formation documents must then be completed on paper rather than through the online formation process.
6What document creates an Iowa LLC?
A domestic Iowa LLC is formed by filing a Certificate of Organization with the Iowa Secretary of State.
7Can I form an Iowa LLC online?
Yes. Iowa provides online LLC formation through its Fast Track Filing system.
8Does Iowa offer expedited LLC filing?
Yes. Iowa currently offers expedited processing for many business filings at additional surcharges: $15 for 5-business-day service, $50 for 2-business-day service, $125 for same-business-day service, or $200 for 1-hour service. These amounts are added to the regular $50 filing fee.
9Does Iowa require an Operating Agreement?
The Secretary of State’s formation process does not require you to file an Operating Agreement with the state. An Operating Agreement can nevertheless be useful for documenting ownership, management, voting, distributions, and other internal rules.
10What is Iowa’s individual income-tax rate in 2026?
Iowa’s individual income-tax rate for tax year 2026 is 3.8% (flat rate).
11What is Iowa’s sales-tax rate?
Iowa’s state sales and use tax rate is 6%. Many cities and counties impose a 1% local option sales tax, so the combined rate can be higher depending on the jurisdiction and transaction. Not every location has the local option tax.
12Does Iowa have a Pass-Through Entity Tax?
Yes. Iowa allows qualifying partnerships and S corporations to elect the Pass-Through Entity Tax. The election is voluntary and is separate from the LLC formation process. A single-member LLC treated as a disregarded entity is not eligible to make its own PTET election.
13Can a non-U.S. resident form an Iowa LLC?
The Iowa Secretary of State’s formation process does not state that every LLC owner must be a U.S. citizen. However, non-U.S. owners can face additional federal tax, EIN, banking, and documentation requirements. A qualified Iowa registered agent is still required. Review the current IRS EIN procedures, banking requirements, and any applicable federal reporting requirements before forming.
