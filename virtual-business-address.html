Virtual Business Address for LLCs (2026 Guide)

Keep Your Home Address Off Public Record & Protect Personal Privacy

When you form an LLC, the state requires a physical address for your principal place of business and registered agent. Using your personal home address exposes your residential location to public records, junk mail, and data scrapers. A virtual business address provides a commercial street address to keep your personal location private while remaining 100% compliant.
