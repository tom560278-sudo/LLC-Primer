ZenBusiness and LegalZoom both offer LLC formation starting at $0 plus state filing fees, but their paid plans are structured differently. ZenBusiness focuses on business formation, compliance and management tools, while LegalZoom combines formation with a broader set of legal and business services, including access to independent attorneys on qualifying plans.

For a straightforward formation, compare the total first-year price, processing speed, registered-agent cost, compliance services, and any add-ons you actually need. If access to attorney services is important, LegalZoom's current Pro and Premium formation packages include a 30-day attorney-service subscription, subject to its terms.

Key Takeaway

ZenBusiness vs LegalZoom is not simply a comparison of $0 formation offers. Its Pro plan is $199/year, Premium $299/year; registered-agent service is $199/year separately. LegalZoom's current structure combines LLC formation with a broader range of legal and business services. Its Basic plan starts at $0, Pro at $249, and Premium at $299, plus state filing fees. Frequently Asked Questions

01 Is ZenBusiness better than LegalZoom?
There is no universal answer. ZenBusiness emphasizes formation, compliance and business-management tools, while LegalZoom combines formation with a broader range of legal and business services, including attorney access on qualifying plans. Compare the services you actually need.
02 Is ZenBusiness really $0?
Yes, ZenBusiness currently advertises a $0 Starter formation package plus state filing fees. Optional services can add to the total cost, and some services renew annually.
03 Is LegalZoom really $0?
Yes. LegalZoom currently lists its Basic LLC formation plan at $0 plus state filing fees. The Basic plan is limited compared with its paid packages, so review the included services before assuming the $0 option covers everything you need.
04 Which company is cheaper?
At the formation-only level, both currently start at $0 plus state filing fees. ZenBusiness's Pro plan is $199/year, while LegalZoom's Pro plan is $249. The services included differ, so the lower price does not by itself establish greater value for a particular business.
05 Does ZenBusiness include a registered agent?
Not in its current standard formation packages. ZenBusiness lists registered-agent service as a $199/year add-on.
06 How much does LegalZoom's registered agent cost?
LegalZoom currently lists its registered-agent service at $249 per year and states that the service auto-renews.
07 Which company offers attorney access?
LegalZoom currently includes attorney-related services with its Pro and Premium LLC formation packages, subject to legal-service terms. ZenBusiness's standard formation packages do not include an equivalent attorney-consultation service.
08 Can I get an EIN for free?
Yes. Eligible applicants can apply for an EIN directly through the IRS without paying a formation company. ZenBusiness lists EIN at $99 as an add-on; LegalZoom lists it at $79 standalone. Both include it in qualifying paid formation packages.
09 Do I need to buy an operating agreement from either company?
Not necessarily. An operating agreement is an internal LLC document and state requirements differ. You can prepare one yourself or use another provider.
10 Can I switch from LegalZoom to ZenBusiness later?
Yes. Your LLC remains the same legal entity. You can stop using one provider and use another for optional services such as registered-agent service, compliance assistance, or other business-management services.
11 Does changing providers change my LLC?
No. The LLC remains registered with the state under its existing legal name and entity record.
12 Does LegalZoom's attorney service mean LegalZoom is my law firm?
No. LegalZoom's attorney-service disclosures explain that the attorneys are independent attorneys. Review the current legal-plan terms to understand the attorney relationship and scope of service.
13 Do ZenBusiness and LegalZoom file my annual report automatically?
It depends on the service or plan you purchase and the state involved. ZenBusiness includes state-required annual report filing through its Worry-Free Compliance service on qualifying plans. Always confirm exactly which filings are included.
14 Which one should I choose?
Start with the service you actually need. If you mainly want formation and business-management tools, compare ZenBusiness's current plans. If attorney access and a broader legal-service platform are important, compare LegalZoom's Pro or Premium offering and its legal-service terms.
15 What if I only need basic formation?
Both have $0 + state fee starting options. You can also form an LLC yourself directly with the state without using either provider.
