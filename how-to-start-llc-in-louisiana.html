How to Start an LLC in Louisiana in 2026

Last reviewed: September 7, 2026  ·  Primary sources: Louisiana Secretary of State (geauxBIZ), Louisiana Department of Revenue, FinCEN

Louisiana LLC formation requires an important step that is easy to miss: notarization. The Articles of Organization and Initial Report must be properly completed and notarized before submission. The standard state filing fee is $100, and the annual report costs $30 on the LLC’s anniversary date.

Louisiana’s tax rules also changed significantly for 2025 and 2026. The state moved to a flat 3% individual income tax rate beginning January 1, 2025, while the corporation franchise tax was eliminated for taxable periods beginning January 1, 2026.

Louisiana’s 2025–2026 Tax Changes at a Glance: (1) Individual income tax: replaced graduated structure (1.85% / 3.5% / 4.25%) with a flat 3% beginning January 1, 2025. (2) Corporation franchise tax: eliminated for taxable periods beginning January 1, 2026. Pass-through LLCs generally did not pay the corporation franchise tax in the same manner as corporations.

Step 1: Choose a Name for Your Louisiana LLC

Your Louisiana LLC name must comply with the state’s naming requirements and be distinguishable from existing business names in the Secretary of State’s records. The name should include an acceptable limited-liability designation such as “Limited Liability Company,” “LLC,” or “L.L.C.” Before filing, search the Louisiana Secretary of State’s business database to check name availability.

Louisiana DBA Difference: Louisiana generally handles trade-name registration through the appropriate parish Clerk of Court, rather than through the Secretary of State in the same manner used by many other states. If your LLC will use a name other than its legal name, check the requirements with the applicable parish Clerk of Court.

Step 2: Appoint a Louisiana Registered Agent

Every Louisiana LLC must have a registered agent and registered office in Louisiana. The registered agent receives official documents, including service of process and certain state notices. A registered agent must have a qualifying physical address in Louisiana — a P.O. Box or out-of-state address does not satisfy the state’s requirement.

Step 3: File the Articles of Organization and Initial Report

Louisiana requires an LLC to file its Articles of Organization with the Secretary of State. The standard filing fee is $100. Louisiana also requires an Initial Report as part of the formation process. File through geauxBIZ, Louisiana’s Secretary of State business portal.

🚨 Notarization Requirement — Easy to Miss: Louisiana’s formation process includes notarization requirements for the Articles of Organization and Initial Report that must be completed before submitting the formation documents. Make sure the required signatures and notarizations are completed correctly before submitting. A filing that does not satisfy the applicable requirements can be rejected, which can delay formation and require correction or resubmission.

Step 4: Create an Operating Agreement

Louisiana does not generally require an LLC operating agreement to be filed with the Secretary of State. However, an operating agreement can establish: ownership percentages, member contributions, voting rights, management authority, profit and loss distributions, member transfers, buyout procedures, adding or removing members, and dissolution procedures. For a multi-member LLC, putting these arrangements in writing can help establish how the company is intended to operate.

Step 5: Apply for an EIN

An EIN is issued by the IRS at no charge. Many LLCs obtain an EIN after formation for banking, tax, and other business purposes. Eligible applicants can apply directly through the IRS online application. Non-U.S. applicants should follow the current IRS instructions for Form SS-4. Do not rely on old formation-service instructions for IRS phone, fax, or mailing procedures. See our EIN guide.

Step 6: Open a Business Bank Account

A separate business bank account can help keep company finances distinct from personal finances. Banks may request: approved Articles of Organization, EIN confirmation, operating agreement, government-issued identification, and ownership information. Requirements vary by financial institution. See our business bank account guide.

Louisiana LLC taxation depends on the LLC’s tax classification and business activity. An LLC may be treated as a disregarded entity, partnership, S corporation, or C corporation for federal tax purposes depending on its ownership and elections. An LLC owner should not interpret the 3% rate as a separate automatic tax on the LLC itself — the actual treatment depends on the company’s tax classification and the owner’s circumstances. Consult a qualified Louisiana tax professional.

Louisiana Annual Report

Louisiana LLCs must file an annual report with the Secretary of State. The current annual report fee is $30. The annual report is generally due on the LLC’s anniversary date (the date the LLC was originally formed). Set a recurring reminder before the anniversary date so the filing does not get overlooked. Failure to maintain required filings can affect the company’s good standing.

Common Mistakes When Starting a Louisiana LLC

Mistake #1 — Ignoring the Notarization Requirements: Louisiana’s formation process includes notarization requirements for the Articles of Organization and Initial Report. Confirm the current Secretary of State instructions and make sure all required signatures and notarizations have been completed correctly before submitting.
Mistake #2 — Forgetting the Anniversary-Date Annual Report: Louisiana does not use one universal annual-report date for every LLC. Your deadline is tied to your company’s formation anniversary. Add the date to your business calendar immediately after formation.
Mistake #3 — Assuming a DBA Is Filed Like Other States: Louisiana handles trade-name requirements through the appropriate parish Clerk of Court, not through the Secretary of State. Check the requirements with the applicable parish.
Mistake #4 — Treating Sales Tax as Only a State-Level Issue: Louisiana sales-tax obligations can involve state and local requirements. Determine the applicable registration, collection, and filing requirements based on where and how your business operates.
BOTTOM LINE

Starting a Louisiana LLC requires more than paying the $100 formation fee. The formation process includes selecting an available name, appointing a registered agent, preparing the Articles of Organization and Initial Report, completing applicable notarization requirements, and submitting through geauxBIZ. After formation, track the $30 annual report due on the LLC’s anniversary date. Louisiana’s individual income-tax rate is now a flat 3%, and the corporation franchise tax was eliminated for taxable periods beginning in 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions

1How much does it cost to start an LLC in Louisiana?
The standard Louisiana Secretary of State filing fee is $100. Additional costs can include notarization, a registered-agent service, name reservation ($25), licenses, and other business expenses.
2Does Louisiana require an operating agreement?
Louisiana does not generally require an operating agreement to be filed with the Secretary of State. An operating agreement can nevertheless establish the LLC’s ownership, management, and internal operating rules.
3How long does it take to form an LLC in Louisiana?
Standard online processing can take approximately 2–3 business days, although processing times can vary. Any preparation and notarization required before submission should also be included in your timeline.
4How much is the Louisiana LLC annual report?
The current Louisiana LLC annual report fee is $30.
5When is the Louisiana LLC annual report due?
The annual report is generally due on the LLC’s anniversary date, based on the date the company was formed. Set a recurring reminder before the anniversary date so the filing does not get overlooked.
6Can I reserve a Louisiana LLC name?
Yes. Louisiana allows an available name to be reserved for 120 days. The current reservation fee is $25.
7Does Louisiana have a franchise tax in 2026?
Louisiana eliminated its corporation franchise tax for taxable periods beginning January 1, 2026. The tax treatment of an individual LLC still depends on its tax classification and circumstances.
8What is Louisiana’s individual income-tax rate in 2026?
Louisiana’s individual income-tax rate is 3% (flat rate) under the rate structure that began January 1, 2025.
9What is Louisiana’s sales tax rate?
Louisiana’s state sales-tax rate is 5%. Applicable local taxes can increase the combined rate. The exact sales-tax obligations depend on the business’s products or services, location, and taxable activity.
10Can a non-U.S. resident form a Louisiana LLC?
A non-U.S. resident can generally be an LLC owner, subject to the applicable formation, registered-agent, tax, and identification requirements. Non-U.S. owners should also review the current IRS requirements for obtaining an EIN.
11What is the notarization requirement for a Louisiana LLC?
Louisiana’s formation process includes notarization requirements for the Articles of Organization and Initial Report. Make sure the required signatures and notarizations are completed correctly before submitting. A filing that does not satisfy these requirements can be rejected and require correction or resubmission.
12How do Louisiana DBA / trade names work?
Louisiana generally handles trade-name registration through the appropriate parish Clerk of Court, rather than through the Secretary of State in the same manner used by many other states. Check the requirements and fees applicable to the parish where the business operates.
