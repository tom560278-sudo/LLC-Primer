US Business Bank Account for Non-US Residents (2026)

Last reviewed: September 7, 2026  ·  Official source: IRS EIN guidance and current platform terms.

Yes, non-US residents can open a US business bank account remotely without visiting the United States, provided they have a US LLC, an EIN, and the required formation documents.

In 2026, the most popular options for foreign-owned LLCs are Mercury, Wise Business, and Relay. Mercury remains the preferred choice for most founders because it does not require an SSN or ITIN, offers a fully online application process, and integrates directly with Stripe.

Most non-residents can expect the entire process to take between 4 and 8 weeks. The bank application itself usually takes only a few days, but obtaining an EIN from the IRS is often the longest step.

Mercury for non-US residents

Mercury is the most widely used US banking platform among non-resident LLC founders. It offers $0 monthly fees, no SSN requirement, strong Stripe integration, and a fully remote application process. It is the default first choice for most international founders in 2026.

Mercury is a fintech company, not a bank. Banking services are provided through Choice Financial Group and Column N.A., both FDIC members. FDIC insurance covers up to $250,000 per depositor.

What Mercury requires from non-residents

  • US LLC or C-Corp legally registered and in good standing
  • EIN — ideally the original CP575 confirmation letter
  • Articles of Organization
  • Operating agreement
  • Valid passport
  • A real US business address (not your RA address)
  • Specific, consistent business description across all fields

Mercury's prohibited-country list

Mercury maintains a list of countries from which it will not open accounts. The list is based on OFAC sanctions, FATF grey lists, and Mercury's own risk policies. Check Mercury's current prohibited countries list directly before applying — it updates independently of FATF status changes. As of early 2026, Mercury had not yet updated its list to reflect Nigeria, Philippines, and Croatia being removed from the FATF greylist in 2026.

Apply once, apply correctly. Mercury's system auto-rejects re-applications from the same LLC. A declined application stays on record. If you're not certain your country qualifies and your documents are complete, contact Mercury support before submitting — they can advise without triggering a formal rejection.

The address issue

Mercury collects two address fields: your personal residential address and your business address. Your personal address is your actual home address outside the US. Your business address must be a real US street address — a virtual office service (iPostal, Regus, Alliance Virtual) works, but your registered agent's address does not qualify as a business address. Confusing these two fields is the most common application mistake for non-residents in 2026.

What Wise actually provides

A Wise Business account gives your LLC a real US routing number and account number. These work exactly like a bank account number for receiving Stripe payouts, Amazon disbursements, PayPal transfers, and ACH payments. Most payment processors cannot tell the difference between a Wise account and a traditional bank account at the routing number level.

What Wise does not provide: FDIC insurance (funds are held in safeguarded accounts separate from Wise's own money, but not insured by the FDIC), the ability to write checks, or access to US Federal Reserve payment rails that require direct bank membership.

For founders whose country of residence is on Mercury's prohibited list, Wise Business is often the primary banking solution — not a fallback. It does the job for most digital businesses that receive USD and need to hold and convert funds internationally.

Relay for non-US residents

Relay is a fintech platform with banking services through Thread Bank (FDIC member). FDIC coverage goes up to $3 million through a sweep program — the highest of any option reviewed here. The accounting integrations (QuickBooks, Xero) are best-in-class.

The critical barrier: Relay requires an SSN or ITIN for non-resident beneficial owners. Non-residents who have obtained an ITIN can apply. Those without either face a hard barrier — Relay will not onboard without one of these identifiers.

Do you need an EIN before applying?

Yes. Every platform on this list requires your EIN before accepting an application. No bank or fintech will open a business account without it. For non-US residents, this is the primary bottleneck.

The EIN is your LLC's federal tax identification number. Without an SSN, you apply via Form SS-4 by fax or by calling the IRS international line. The IRS processes non-resident EIN applications in 3–6 weeks in 2026 due to ongoing staffing constraints.

This means your realistic timeline from LLC formation to an active bank account is approximately 3–4 months:

  • LLC formation: 1–3 business days (Wyoming)
  • EIN processing: 3–6 weeks
  • Bank application and approval: 1–5 business days

Don't wait until you "need" banking to start this process. Start the EIN application the day your LLC is approved. For the complete non-resident EIN application process, see our EIN guide for non-US residents, or the general EIN for LLC guide for context.

Request your EIN confirmation (CP575) by having it sent to your registered agent's address — that's a US address the IRS can mail to. When your EIN arrives, use it immediately for bank applications. The original CP575 letter carries more weight with banking compliance teams than a copy or a 147c letter.

Why applications get rejected — and what to do about it

Bank rejections for non-resident LLCs in 2026 have a predictable set of causes. Most are avoidable.

Incomplete or inconsistent documents

Your name must appear exactly the same way across your passport, Articles of Organization, operating agreement, and EIN letter. Middle names, suffixes, and transliterations create flags. If you use "Mohammad" on your passport and "Mohammed" in your LLC documents, get a name correction on one of them before applying.

Wrong address

Using your registered agent's address as your business address is the most common Mercury rejection trigger in 2026. You need a real business address — a virtual office from a legitimate provider works. A PO box or mail forwarding address without a street number does not.

Prohibited country of residence

Mercury, Wise, and AirWallex all maintain restricted-country lists. Living in a restricted country while applying — even if your LLC is properly formed — results in an automatic rejection. The list changes independently of FATF status. Always check the platform's current list directly before submitting.

High-risk industry classification

Industries that trigger enhanced compliance review include: crypto and digital assets, gambling and gaming, firearms and ammunition, adult content, money transfer services, and pharmaceutical sales. This doesn't mean automatic rejection, but applications in these categories face longer review periods and more documentation requests.

Vague business description

"Online business" or "consulting" is not a sufficient business description. Banks use the description to verify that the business activity matches the transaction patterns they'll see. "B2B SaaS product for e-commerce inventory management, selling to US and European businesses, $3,000–$15,000 annual contracts" is the level of specificity that passes compliance review.

Missing EIN or wrong document format

Some non-residents start their EIN arrives using it as "proof of application" — this doesn't work. You need the actual EIN. The CP575 letter is the strongest document. If you've lost it, request a 147c letter from the IRS first, then apply.

No business website or digital presence

Not a hard requirement for Mercury, but applications without a business website, LinkedIn presence, or any verifiable online footprint are more likely to go into extended compliance review. A simple landing page explaining what the business does and who it serves reduces this risk significantly.

Re-application after rejection

Mercury auto-rejects re-applications. One strike. If your Mercury application was rejected and the issue was fixable (wrong address, missing document), contact Mercury support directly before submitting again — they can sometimes clear the application for re-review. For Wise and Relay, check their official support articles for their re-application policies.

A rejected application does not affect your LLC's standing — it only affects your ability to bank with that specific institution. If Mercury rejects you, your LLC is still valid. Move to Wise Business as your primary option and address the rejection reason before attempting Mercury again.

Best setup by founder type

There's no universal answer — the right combination depends on your country, business model, and transaction volume.

Freelancer or consultant

Your priority is receiving USD payments from US clients and minimizing conversion fees. Start with Mercury as your primary account. Add Wise Business to receive international payments in multiple currencies. Stripe links to Mercury. Total fee: $0/month.

E-commerce seller (Amazon, Shopify, Etsy)

You need an account that Amazon, PayPal, and Stripe can pay into, plus the ability to handle international expenses. Mercury handles USD payouts cleanly. Wise Business handles international supplier payments with better exchange rates. Run both from the start.

SaaS founder

Stripe integration is critical. Mercury is the strongest Stripe partner and the right primary account. If you expect significant revenue from non-US markets, add Wise to receive multi-currency payments without conversion friction. If you eventually get an ITIN, Relay's accounting integrations simplify QuickBooks sync.

Agency owner with multiple clients

You need clean bookkeeping from day one. Mercury works well with basic accounting setups. If you're managing 10+ clients with different payment currencies, Wise Business as your primary account simplifies the multi-currency side. Add Relay later once you have an ITIN — its sub-account system is the best for tracking client-specific cash flow.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a non-US resident open a US business bank account?+
Yes. Mercury, Wise Business, Relay, and AirWallex all support fully remote account opening for non-US residents. You verify identity online, submit documents digitally, and receive account details within 1–5 business days without visiting the United States. Nova and Found require an SSN and are not available to non-residents.
Do I need an SSN to open a US business bank account?+
No, not for Mercury or Wise Business. Mercury is the most popular option for non-residents precisely because it does not require an SSN or ITIN. Relay requires an SSN or ITIN from beneficial owners. Traditional banks like Chase and Bank of America generally require an SSN for remote applications.
Do I need an ITIN to open a US business bank account?+
Not for Mercury or Wise Business. Relay requires an ITIN (or SSN) from non-US beneficial owners. If you don't have an ITIN and don't plan to get one, start with Mercury. An ITIN is a personal tax ID issued by the IRS — it's a separate process from getting an EIN and takes several months to obtain.
Can I open a US business bank account remotely without visiting the US?+
Yes, through Mercury, Wise Business, Relay, and AirWallex. All support fully remote identity verification (video or photo check) and account opening. Traditional banks — Chase, Bank of America, Citibank — require in-person branch visits. Wells Fargo is the most foreigner-friendly traditional bank for in-person visits but is not available remotely.
Which bank is best for a foreign-owned LLC?+
Mercury is the best primary account for most non-residents: $0 fees, no SSN or ITIN required, strongest Stripe integration, and fully remote opening. If Mercury is unavailable in your country, Wise Business is the best alternative. If you have an ITIN and need advanced accounting integrations, Relay is worth considering. Use Mercury + Wise together for maximum flexibility.
Can I use Mercury without visiting the US?+
Yes. Mercury's entire application and account management process is fully remote. You verify your identity via a video or photo check through their onboarding platform, submit all documents digitally, and manage your account online. Mercury does not require any in-person verification or US presence.
