5. Privacy by Default: How It Works

Privacy protection is one of the more useful parts of Northwest's service, and it's built into the standard plan rather than sold as an add-on.

What Happens Without Privacy Protection

State law requires a public filing when forming an LLC. That filing, the Articles of Organization, typically includes the owner's name, address, and phone number, depending on the state's exact requirements. Once that information sits in a public database, data brokers commonly scrape and resell it. Junk mail, spam calls, and a home address turning up in searches tied to the business name are the common downstream effects. None of that can be undone for the original filing once it's public — switching registered agents later stops further exposure, but it doesn't erase what's already on record.

How Northwest Approaches This

Northwest can use its own address instead of the client's on state filings, in the states and filing types where that option applies, and it's included in the base plan at no separate fee. As a result, clients get a usable business address for LLC purposes bundled into the $39 formation fee. It's worth noting that a virtual address vs registered agent aren't interchangeable — they serve different legal purposes, though Northwest's address can cover both functions within its service.

7. The Corporate Guides® Experience

Most LLC services offer customer support of some kind, though the quality behind that support varies widely. Northwest calls its team Corporate Guides® — formation specialists rather than general call-center staff, and that distinction shapes the kind of help you actually get on the phone.

What Sets Corporate Guides Apart

Corporate Guides are trained in entity types, state-specific filing requirements, and LLC annual report deadlines. Because of that training, they can typically handle a question like "Should I form in Wyoming or my home state?" with more depth than a general support queue would offer. They're also not paid on commission, which reduces the incentive to push add-ons that a caller may not need.

A Real Limitation Worth Knowing

Support runs by phone and email, Monday through Friday, with no live chat and no 24/7 coverage. That's a genuine drawback if you need help on a weekend. Anyone still unsure whether an LLC is even the right structure can try the free business structure quiz before picking up the phone.

9. Using Northwest for an Existing LLC

Northwest isn't only for new formations. An LLC that already exists, wherever it was originally formed, can switch to Northwest as its registered agent, and the change-of-agent filing is handled at no extra cost.

The Transfer Process

Northwest prepares the change-of-agent paperwork and files it with the state to update the registered agent of record. Timing typically runs to a matter of days, though the exact figure depends on the state.

ℹ️ Can I Be My Own Registered Agent? Most states allow LLC owners to act as their own registered agent. The trade-off: your address appears on public records, you need to be available during business hours at that address, and you personally receive anything served in person. For many owners, paying for a professional RA service is worth it for privacy and convenience alone.
