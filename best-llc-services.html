Best Registered Agent Services

Most states require an LLC to maintain a registered agent. Some formation services include registered-agent service for a limited period, while others charge a separate annual fee. Compare registered-agent services by pricing, coverage, document handling, and privacy features.

ℹ️ WHAT IS A REGISTERED AGENT?

A registered agent is a person or company designated to receive service of process and certain official state correspondence on behalf of an LLC. Requirements vary by state. Read our full Registered Agent Guide for state rules and available options.

Frequently Asked Questions

1 What should I consider when choosing an LLC formation service?
Compare the formation price, state fees, registered-agent costs, renewal pricing, included documents, processing options, and support. Different services offer different features, so the right option depends on your needs.
2 Do I need an LLC formation service?
No. You can generally file directly with your state. A formation service is optional and may handle filing paperwork or provide additional services such as a registered agent or operating agreement.
3 How much do LLC formation services cost?
Many services offer a $0 formation option, where you pay the state's filing fee separately. Paid plans and add-ons can increase the total cost. Always check the current plan, renewal pricing, and state fees before purchasing.
4 What does LegalZoom offer?
LegalZoom offers LLC formation along with additional legal and business services. Registered-agent service, attorney access, and other features depend on the plan selected and may involve additional costs.
5 What is the difference between ZenBusiness and Northwest?
ZenBusiness offers a $0 formation option with formation and compliance services. Northwest starts at $39 + state fees and focuses on formation and registered-agent services. Compare current pricing, renewals, included features, and registered-agent terms.
6 Which LLC service is available for non-U.S. founders?
Several providers offer services for international founders. doola specifically offers U.S. business formation services for non-U.S. founders, with options such as registered-agent, EIN, and banking assistance. Requirements can vary based on the founder and state.
