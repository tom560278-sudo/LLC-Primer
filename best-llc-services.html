ℹ️ WHAT IS A REGISTERED AGENT?

A registered agent is a person or company designated to receive legal documents, tax notices, and government correspondence on behalf of your LLC. Every state requires one. Read our full Registered Agent Guide to learn state rules and options.

Frequently Asked Questions

1 What is the best LLC formation service?
For most business owners, Northwest Registered Agent is our top recommendation due to its $39 flat pricing, free first-year registered agent, privacy protection, and transparent pricing without hidden renewal surprises. ZenBusiness is another excellent choice for $0 formation with guided compliance.
2 Do I need an LLC formation service?
No. You can file Articles of Organization directly with your state Secretary of State office. However, using a formation service saves time, ensures accuracy, handles registered agent requirements, and provides templates like Operating Agreements and compliance reminders.
3 How much do LLC formation services cost?
Base formation services range from $0 to $297 plus state filing fees. Many services offer $0 base packages (you only pay state fees), but add-on services like registered agent ($119–$249/yr), EIN filing ($50–$99), and Operating Agreements can increase the total cost.
4 Is LegalZoom worth it?
LegalZoom is a recognized legal brand and is worth considering if you need access to ongoing attorney consultations or estate planning bundle packages. However, for standard LLC formation, it is more expensive than competitors like Northwest or ZenBusiness and charges higher ongoing registered agent fees ($249/yr).
5 What is the difference between ZenBusiness and Northwest?
ZenBusiness offers a $0 base formation plan with guided compliance tools, but charges $199/yr separately for registered agent service. Northwest charges $39 for formation, includes 1 year of registered agent service for free, provides superior address privacy, and does not require annual subscriptions.
6 Which LLC service is best for non-US founders?
doola ($297 + state) is specifically built for non-US founders and non-residents, offering full remote LLC setup, EIN acquisition without SSN, US business bank account assistance, and ongoing tax filings. Northwest is also popular for non-US founders who want a lower-cost $39 entry point in Wyoming or Delaware.
