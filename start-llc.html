6-STEP FORMATION ROADMAP

The Complete Master LLC Roadmap

Forming a Limited Liability Company (LLC) protects your personal savings, house, and assets from business liabilities. Follow these 6 essential steps to launch your business correctly.

01

Choose Your State

Home State vs Domestic vs Non-Resident

If you operate a physical business in your home state (e.g. retail, local services), file in your home state. For online businesses or privacy, states like Wyoming ($100) or Delaware ($140) are popular alternatives.

Check 50-State Filing Fees →
02

LLC Name Search

State Registry Distinguishability

Your business name must be unique on your Secretary of State's business registry and include an official designator like "LLC" or "L.L.C.". Conduct a free name search on the Secretary of State portal.

✓ Free State Registry Name Checks
03

Designate Registered Agent

Legal Notice & Privacy Shield

Every state requires a registered agent with a physical street address in the formation state. You can serve as your own agent for $0, or hire a commercial service (~$125/yr) to keep your personal address off public state records.

✓ Address Privacy Protection Available
04

File Articles of Organization

Official State Registration

Submit your legal filing (Articles of Organization or Certificate of Formation) directly to the Secretary of State. State filing fees range from $35 (MT) to $500 (MA).

Estimate Total State Fees →
05

Draft Operating Agreement

Internal Governance Rules

An Operating Agreement defines owner percentages, voting rights, and profit distributions. Even single-member LLCs need an Operating Agreement to open business bank accounts and reinforce liability protection.

✓ Free Member Contract Blueprint
06

Obtain EIN & BOI Report

Federal Tax ID & FinCEN Compliance

Apply for your Employer Identification Number (EIN) for $0 free directly on IRS.gov. Additionally, complete your federal FinCEN Beneficial Ownership Information (BOI) filing within required deadlines.

✓ 100% Free Direct IRS Route

KNOWLEDGE BASE

Frequently Asked Guidance Questions

Can I file an LLC myself without paying third-party filing fees? +
Yes! You can file your Articles of Organization directly through your state's Secretary of State online portal. You only pay the mandatory government state filing fee.
What is the difference between a Single-Member and Multi-Member LLC? +
A Single-Member LLC has one owner (member) and is treated as a disregarded entity by default for tax purposes. A Multi-Member LLC has two or more owners and is taxed as a partnership by default. Both offer full personal liability protection.
Should non-U.S. residents form an LLC in Wyoming or Delaware? +
Wyoming is generally preferred for online businesses and e-commerce founders due to lower annual fees ($60) and zero state income tax. Delaware is preferred if you plan to seek U.S. venture capital or investor funding.
How long does state LLC approval take? +
Online filings in states like Colorado, Nevada, or Wyoming are approved instantly or within 1 business day. Other states may take 3 to 7 business days unless expedited processing is selected.
