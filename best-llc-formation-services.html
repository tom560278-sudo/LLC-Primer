Before You Choose an LLC Service

Before comparing providers, make sure you've answered three foundational questions. The right formation strategy can matter more than the difference between two service providers.

3 Questions to Answer Before You Pay Anything
1
Do you actually need an LLC?

An LLC can provide liability protection and tax-classification flexibility, but it is not the only business structure available. Freelancers, independent contractors, and other small-business owners may have different considerations. Use our Business Structure Quiz to review the basic differences before forming anything.

Once you're ready, check whether your intended business name is available using our free LLC name availability checker.

2
Which state should you form in?

For many owners, forming in the state where the business is actually located and operated is the most straightforward approach. Forming in another state can create additional registration, reporting, or tax obligations if you still conduct business in your home state. Our state formation guide explains the factors to consider before choosing where to form. You can also see our complete how to start an LLC guide for the formation process.

3
What will the full first-year cost be?

The formation service price is only one part of the total. Your actual cost may include: Service fee + state filing fee + Registered Agent + optional documents/services + ongoing state requirements. State filing fees vary considerably by jurisdiction. Use our LLC cost calculator to estimate your state-specific costs before choosing a provider.

When You Should NOT Use an LLC Formation Service

An LLC formation service can simplify the filing process, but it is not required in every situation. If you understand your state's filing requirements and are comfortable submitting the formation documents yourself, you can generally file directly with the state and avoid a separate provider formation fee. The right approach depends on how much assistance you need and which services you actually intend to use.

Skip the Formation Service If...
✗
You're comfortable filing directly with your state

Many states provide online LLC formation portals that allow founders to prepare and submit formation documents directly. If your state's process is straightforward and you are comfortable handling the paperwork yourself, a formation service may not add much value. You would still be responsible for the applicable state filing fee and any state-specific requirements. Our full how to start an LLC guide walks through the self-filing process.

✗
You only need the state filing

If you already understand the formation process and only need to submit Articles of Organization or the equivalent state document, filing directly may be sufficient. You can separately obtain services such as a registered agent service, if needed, rather than purchasing a broader formation package.

✗
Your state has additional formation costs

Some states impose requirements beyond the basic formation filing. For example, New York has a publication requirement for many newly formed LLCs, while publication-related requirements and costs can vary by state and circumstances. These government or third-party costs are generally separate from a formation provider's service fee. Review the applicable state requirements and our LLC cost guide before calculating your total startup cost.

✗
You need legal or tax advice alongside formation

A formation service can help prepare or submit standard formation documents, but that does not make it a substitute for individualized legal or tax advice. If your LLC will involve real estate, multiple investors, different membership interests, regulated activities, significant contracts, or other complex issues, consider obtaining advice from an appropriately qualified attorney or tax professional. A formation service can still be used for administrative filing after the underlying structure has been addressed.

Frequently Asked Questions

1 What is the cheapest LLC formation service in 2026?
Several providers advertise $0 formation plus applicable state fees on their basic plans, including ZenBusiness Starter and Bizee Basic. The total cost can differ once registered-agent service, operating agreements, EIN assistance, compliance products, and other optional services are included. Bizee's advertised formation offer includes the first year of registered-agent service, while Northwest advertises a $39 formation fee with the first year of registered-agent service included. Compare both first-year and renewal costs rather than looking only at the formation fee. Use our LLC cost calculator to compare the major costs for your state.
2 Is it worth paying for an LLC formation service, or can I do it myself?
You can generally form an LLC yourself by filing the required formation document directly with your state. This can eliminate a separate provider formation fee, although you still pay the applicable state filing fee and remain responsible for meeting state requirements. Formation services can provide administrative convenience, document preparation, registered-agent services, reminders, and other optional features. Whether those services are worth the additional cost depends on how much assistance you want. See our how to start an LLC guide for a step-by-step self-filing overview.
3 Does it matter which state I form my LLC in?
Yes. Your formation state can affect filing fees, recurring reports or taxes, privacy rules, and other compliance requirements. If you conduct business primarily in your home state, forming there is often the more straightforward option. Forming in another state while actually operating elsewhere can create additional foreign-registration, reporting, tax, or fee obligations. The appropriate choice depends on the business and the states involved. See our LLC formation state guide for factors to consider when comparing states.
4 What is the best website to form an LLC in 2026?
There is no single formation website that has the same advantages for every business. Providers differ in formation pricing, registered-agent costs, document packages, processing options, compliance services, support, and recurring charges.

For example, Northwest advertises a $39 formation fee plus the applicable state fee and includes first-year registered-agent service under its formation offer. Bizee and ZenBusiness advertise $0 basic formation options plus applicable state fees, while their additional services and renewal terms differ. LegalZoom combines LLC formation with a broader range of legal and business services.

Before choosing a provider, compare the complete cost structure and included services, not just the advertised formation price. You can also check your business name using our free LLC name availability checker before starting the filing process.
5 What is a registered agent and do I need one?
A registered agent is a person or company designated to receive service of process and certain official state correspondence for an LLC. LLC registered-agent requirements vary by state, but LLCs generally must maintain an eligible registered agent while active.

A registered agent generally needs a qualifying physical address in the state and must meet that state's availability and eligibility requirements. You can often serve as your own registered agent if you qualify, or use a commercial service. A commercial registered agent may also provide convenience and, where permitted, allow the provider's address to be used for registered-agent records. See our full registered agent guide for state-specific considerations.
6 Is ZenBusiness or Northwest better?
The two providers use different pricing structures and service models, so the comparison depends on which features you need. Northwest advertises a $39 formation fee plus state fees with first-year registered-agent service included, while ZenBusiness offers a $0 basic formation option plus state fees and provides additional plans and recurring services.

Their registered-agent pricing also differs: Northwest lists $125/year after its included first year, while ZenBusiness lists $199/year where registered-agent service is purchased separately based on the pricing reviewed for this guide. ZenBusiness also offers a broader dashboard and business-tool environment, while Northwest emphasizes its registered-agent and support services.

For a detailed side-by-side comparison, see our Northwest vs. ZenBusiness comparison.
7 Does a formation service file my taxes?
Generally, no. LLC formation services primarily handle business-formation and related administrative services, not your ongoing federal and state tax returns. Some providers offer tax consultations, accounting services, or referrals to tax professionals, but those are separate services.

After forming an LLC, you may need an EIN, state tax registrations, tax elections, payroll registrations, or federal and state tax filings depending on your business structure and activities. Tax obligations vary, so consider consulting a qualified tax professional for advice specific to your business. See our post-formation checklist for the main steps to review after formation.
8 What should I do immediately after my LLC is approved?
After your LLC is approved, review the requirements that apply to your business and state. Common next steps can include obtaining an EIN when required or appropriate, creating or adopting an operating agreement, opening a dedicated business bank account, separating business and personal finances, obtaining required licenses or permits, and tracking state reporting deadlines.

Some LLCs also need additional federal or state registrations depending on their activities, ownership, employees, or tax classification. Do not assume every LLC has the same post-formation requirements.
