Before You Choose an LLC Service

Before comparing prices and features, make sure you've answered three foundational questions. Choosing the wrong formation strategy — not the wrong service — is the most common and most costly mistake. Getting these right first means you'll know exactly what to look for in a service and avoid paying for features you don't need.

3 Questions to Answer Before You Pay Anything
1
Do you actually need an LLC?

An LLC makes sense for liability protection, tax flexibility, and credibility. However, freelancers with low risk and sole contractors sometimes do fine as sole proprietors. Use our Business Structure Quiz to confirm the right entity before forming anything. Once you're ready, also check whether your intended business name is available — use our free LLC name availability checker before you pay any formation fee.

2
Which state should you form in?

In most cases, you should form in the state where you live and operate. Forming in Wyoming or Delaware when you're based in California still requires a foreign LLC registration in California — meaning you pay fees in two states. Our best state to form an LLC guide covers every scenario. Also see our full guide on how to start an LLC for the step-by-step process.

3
What will the full first-year cost be?

Service fee + state fee + registered agent + EIN + operating agreement = your real cost. State filing fees alone range from $40 (Kentucky) to $500 (Massachusetts). Use our LLC cost calculator to get your state-specific total before choosing a service — it takes 60 seconds.

When You Should NOT Use an LLC Formation Service

LLC formation services add real value in many situations — but they're not the right answer for everyone. In some cases, using a service adds cost without adding value. Knowing when to skip a service (and file directly with your state) can save you $39–$399 in unnecessary fees. Here are the situations where a formation service is genuinely not the best choice.

Skip the Formation Service If...
✗
You're in a simple, low-risk state with a straightforward filing process

States like Wyoming, New Mexico, and Montana have simple online filing portals, low fees, and no publication requirements. If you're comfortable filling out a basic form and paying a state fee directly, you may not need a service at all. Our full how to start an LLC guide walks you through the self-filing process step by step.

✗
You only need formation — and you already know how to handle the rest

Formation services add the most value when you're new to business and need guidance on operating agreements, EINs, and compliance. If you've formed an LLC before and just need paperwork filed, you're probably paying for features you'll never use. Consider filing directly and using a standalone registered agent service separately — it's often cheaper.

✗
You're in a state with a publication requirement and haven't budgeted for it

New York, Arizona, and Nebraska require new LLCs to publish formation notices in local newspapers — a cost that can run $400-$2,000 and is entirely separate from any formation service fee. No service covers this cost. If you're forming in these states, factor in the publication cost before choosing a service. See our LLC cost guide for state-specific totals.

✗
You need complex legal or tax advice alongside formation

Even LegalZoom — the most legal-forward service here — provides limited attorney access (typically one 30-minute consultation). If you're forming an LLC to hold real estate, bring in investors, issue membership units with different classes, or navigate a regulated industry, a business attorney is the right resource — not a formation service. Use a formation service after the legal structure is sorted, not as a substitute for that guidance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Cost and Value

What is the cheapest LLC formation service in 2026? ▼
Bizee offers a $0 Basic plan that includes a free year of registered agent service. Northwest charges $39 + state fee and includes a free year of registered agent service, an operating agreement, and privacy protection. Over 1-2 years, Northwest is often cheaper overall because of lower registered agent renewal costs and included features.
Is it worth paying for an LLC formation service, or can I do it myself? ▼
If you are comfortable filling out state forms and serving as your own registered agent (listing your home address publicly), you can file directly with your Secretary of State to save $39-$199. However, paying $39 for Northwest gives you address privacy, an operating agreement template, and expert corporate guide support.
Does it matter which state I form my LLC in? ▼
Yes. In 95% of cases, you should form your LLC in your home state where you live and conduct business. Forming in Wyoming or Delaware when operating in another state creates double filing fees and compliance requirements.
What is the best website to form an LLC in 2026? ▼
Northwest Registered Agent is our #1 pick for value, privacy, and zero upsells. ZenBusiness is #2 for founders who want a modern digital dashboard experience.

Services and Features

What is a registered agent and do I need one? ▼
A Registered Agent is a person or entity designated to receive official state correspondence and legal notices (service of process) for your business. Every US LLC is legally required to have one.
Is ZenBusiness or Northwest better? ▼
Northwest is better for lower true costs, privacy protection, and zero upsells. ZenBusiness is better for founders who want an intuitive mobile app and tech-first dashboard experience.
Does a formation service file my taxes? ▼
No. Formation services handle filing your Articles of Organization and obtaining an EIN. You will still need a CPA or tax software to file annual state and federal tax returns.
What should I do immediately after my LLC is approved? ▼
Immediately obtain an EIN from the IRS, open a dedicated business bank account, draft your Operating Agreement, and check local business license requirements.
