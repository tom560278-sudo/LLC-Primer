Privacy Considerations

A registered agent can help keep your personal address separate from the registered-agent address shown on applicable public records. It does not make an LLC anonymous or prevent every personal or business address from appearing in public records. Northwest specifically markets its formation and registered-agent services around privacy. However, LLC owners should understand which addresses their state requires and which records are publicly accessible.

When LegalZoom May Make Sense

1. You Want Attorney Access

LegalZoom's Pro and Premium formation packages currently include a 30-day Business Attorney Plan with unlimited 30-minute consultations on new legal topics. After the included period, the plan can automatically renew at the then-current monthly price unless canceled. This can be relevant if you expect questions involving contracts, business documents, intellectual property, or other legal issues.

2. You Want More Than LLC Formation

LegalZoom offers services beyond forming an LLC, including registered-agent services, business documents, trademarks, and other legal products. If you prefer using one established platform for several business or personal legal needs, its broader product range may be relevant.

3. You Prefer a $0 Starting Price

LegalZoom's Basic formation package starts at $0 plus state filing fees. It is a legitimate option for someone who wants the core formation filing without purchasing a larger package. However, compare the actual features you need before assuming the $0 package is equivalent to Northwest's $39 service.

4. You Want a Larger Legal-Service Ecosystem

LegalZoom has operated for more than two decades and offers a much broader range of legal and business products than a basic LLC formation service. That does not make it automatically better for every LLC. It simply means its platform may be more relevant to owners who expect to use additional legal services.

Key Takeaway

Northwest and LegalZoom use different approaches to LLC formation. Northwest's current $39 + state fee formation service bundles one year of registered agent service and business identity tools, while LegalZoom's $0 Basic package focuses on the core formation process and lets customers purchase additional services separately. LegalZoom becomes more relevant if you specifically want attorney access or other legal services. Its Pro package currently costs $249 plus state fees and includes a 30-day attorney subscription that can renew at $49/month if not canceled. For a straightforward LLC formation, compare the total checkout price, registered-agent terms, included services, and recurring subscriptions.

Frequently Asked Questions

01 Is Northwest cheaper than LegalZoom?
It depends on which services you compare. Northwest's formation service is currently $39 + state fees, and the first year of registered agent service is included. LegalZoom's Basic formation starts at $0 + state fees, but its registered agent service is currently listed separately at $249/year.
02 Does LegalZoom include a Registered Agent?
LegalZoom's registered agent service is currently listed as a separate $249/year service. It is not included with the $0 Basic formation package.
03 Does Northwest include a Registered Agent?
Yes. Northwest currently states that its $39 formation service includes one full year of registered agent service. After that first year, Northwest currently lists the registered-agent renewal price at $125 per year.
04 Is LegalZoom worth it for attorney access?
It can be useful if you specifically want access to business attorneys. LegalZoom's current Pro and Premium formation packages include a 30-day Business Attorney Plan. The service automatically renews at the stated monthly rate after the included period unless canceled.
05 Does Northwest have a $0 plan like LegalZoom?
No. Northwest currently charges $39 + state fees for its formation service. However, that service includes one year of registered-agent service and a business identity package.
06 Does LegalZoom include an operating agreement?
LegalZoom currently lists its operating agreement at $99 as a standalone service. Its Pro formation package includes an operating agreement as part of the package.
07 Does LegalZoom include an EIN?
LegalZoom currently lists EIN service at $79 when purchased separately. Its Pro formation package includes the EIN service.
08 Can I switch from LegalZoom to Northwest?
Yes. You can generally change your registered agent by following the applicable state procedure. State filing requirements and fees can vary.
09 Do I need a commercial registered agent?
Not necessarily. In many states, an eligible individual or business can serve as its own registered agent if the statutory requirements are met.
10 Are state filing fees included?
No. The advertised formation prices from both companies are separate from the state's LLC filing fee.
11 Can a registered agent keep my home address completely private?
Not necessarily. A registered agent can keep its address on the registered-agent record, but other LLC filings and public records may require additional addresses.
12 What is Privacy by Default at Northwest?
Privacy by Default® refers to Northwest's approach of using its business address where legally permitted rather than automatically placing a customer's home address in the registered-agent role.
13 Which is better for a first LLC?
It depends on what you want help with. For straightforward formation and registered-agent support, Northwest is more focused on those tasks. For formation combined with attorney access or other legal services, LegalZoom may be more relevant.
14 Which for attorney access needs?
LegalZoom Pro and Premium currently include attorney-related services. ZenBusiness and Northwest do not include equivalent attorney-consultation services in their standard formation packages.
