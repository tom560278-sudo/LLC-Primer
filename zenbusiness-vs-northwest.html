Trying to decide between ZenBusiness vs Northwest Registered Agent? These are two of the most popular LLC formation services on the market right now — and for good reason. Both offer affordable formation options, but only Northwest includes registered agent service for the first year with the $39 formation offer.

But they're built for different types of business owners. ZenBusiness is a tech-driven platform with a free tier and tons of add-ons. Northwest is a privacy-first company with transparent pricing and fewer checkout add-ons. In this comparison, we break down exactly where each service wins — and where it falls short — so you can pick the right one for your LLC.

ZenBusiness vs Northwest — Which Service Wins by State?

Both services form LLCs in all 50 states, but the right choice depends on your specific state's privacy exposure, compliance requirements, and long-term cost profile. Consequently, different states tip the decision in opposite directions.

States Where Northwest's Privacy and Low Renewal Cost Win

Northwest can place its address in the registered agent field; other public address fields still depend on the state and the information submitted. In these states, therefore, that protection is especially valuable — either because public records are heavily indexed or because long-term renewal savings are most significant.

Oregon LLC Formation Guide — $100 filing fee, $100/yr annual report
Northwest may reduce exposure in the registered agent field, but Oregon can require other public address information.
Starting an LLC in Utah — $54 filing fee, $18/yr
Utah's minimal ongoing costs mean the RA renewal rate is the dominant long-term expense; therefore, Northwest's $125/yr versus ZenBusiness's $199/yr saves $370 over five years on what is otherwise a low-cost state.
Minnesota LLC Formation Guide — $155 filing fee, $0/yr ongoing
Minnesota requires an annual renewal but currently charges no filing fee; moreover, Northwest's zero-upsell checkout and Privacy by Default make it the cleaner choice for owners who simply want the filing done without managing auto-renewal subscriptions afterward.
Starting an LLC in Michigan — $50 filing fee, $25/yr annual statement
Michigan's low ongoing costs make the RA renewal the primary annual expense; as a result, Northwest's $125/yr renewal versus ZenBusiness's $199/yr Worry-Free Compliance auto-renewal creates a meaningful cost difference over time.

States Where ZenBusiness's Dashboard and $0 Entry Win

ZenBusiness's compliance dashboard and $0 Starter plan deliver the most value in states with active annual filing requirements and growing entrepreneurial communities. In these states, however, privacy concerns are lower and the dashboard's tracking tools justify the trade-off.

Ohio LLC Formation Guide — $99 filing fee, $0/yr ongoing
Ohio has no annual report requirement, which limits the value of compliance tracking; nevertheless, ZenBusiness's $0 entry price is hard to beat for Ohio first-timers who want a guided formation experience without upfront cost.
Starting an LLC in Tennessee — $300 filing fee, $300/yr annual report
Tennessee's high annual report fee makes ZenBusiness's compliance reminders genuinely useful; furthermore, missing the deadline triggers penalties that the automated calendar directly prevents for first-time owners unfamiliar with the state's strict schedule.
Pennsylvania LLC Formation Guide — $125 filing fee, $7 annual report
Pennsylvania's tech corridor between Philadelphia and Pittsburgh has a high concentration of digital entrepreneurs; consequently, ZenBusiness's modern dashboard and Velo AI assistant match that audience's preference for software-first business tools.
Starting an LLC in New Jersey — $125 filing fee, $75/yr annual report
New Jersey's dense business environment near New York City generates significant first-time LLC formation activity; moreover, ZenBusiness's $0 Starter plan reduces the upfront barrier for entrepreneurs testing a new business idea before committing to higher ongoing costs.
KEY TAKEAWAY

The ZenBusiness vs Northwest decision depends on your priorities. Want the cheapest entry price? Go with ZenBusiness at $0. Want privacy, speed, and lower long-term costs? Pick Northwest at $39. Both will file your LLC correctly. The difference is in the extras and the experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

01 Is ZenBusiness really free?
ZenBusiness's Starter plan is $0 + state fees. However, it does not include Registered Agent service ($199/yr), an Operating Agreement ($99), or an EIN ($99).
02 Is Northwest Registered Agent legit?
Yes, Northwest Registered Agent has been in business for over 20 years and has filed hundreds of thousands of business entities across all 50 U.S. states. They maintain physical offices in every state.
03 Which is cheaper long-term: ZenBusiness or Northwest?
Northwest is significantly cheaper long-term. Northwest includes the first year of Registered Agent service free and renews at $125/year. ZenBusiness charges $199/year for Registered Agent service from day one, plus optional $199/year Worry-Free Compliance fees.
04 Does ZenBusiness sell my data?
ZenBusiness shares customer information with third-party partners for partner offers and marketing promotions unless you opt out. Northwest, by contrast, has a strict "Privacy by Default" guarantee and never sells customer data.
05 Can I get an EIN for free instead of paying for it?
Yes! You can apply for an Employer Identification Number (EIN) directly from the IRS website for free in under 15 minutes. Both ZenBusiness and Northwest offer paid EIN filing add-ons if you prefer them to handle it.
