Trying to decide between ZenBusiness vs Northwest Registered Agent? These are two of the most popular LLC formation services on the market right now — and for good reason. Both offer affordable formation options, but only Northwest includes registered agent service for the first year with the $39 formation offer.

But they're built for different types of business owners. ZenBusiness is a tech-driven platform with a free tier and tons of add-ons. Northwest is a privacy-first company with transparent pricing and fewer checkout add-ons. In this comparison, we break down exactly where each service wins — and where it falls short — so you can pick the right one for your LLC.

Independent reputation snapshot: Trustpilot currently lists ZenBusiness at 4.8/5 from 32,424 reviews and Northwest at 4.1/5 from 295 reviews. Review volume differs substantially, so treat these ratings as one signal rather than a direct service test.

Year 2+ Renewal Costs

The first year is cheap with both services. But what happens when you renew? This is where the real cost difference shows up.

  • ZenBusiness: Registered agent service renews at $199/year. If you subscribed to their Worry-Free Compliance, that's another $199/year. Total ongoing cost: $199–$398/year + state fees.
  • Northwest: Registered agent service renews at $125/year. Compliance reminders are included free. Total ongoing cost: $125/year + state fees.

10. Frequently Asked Questions

Is ZenBusiness really free?
ZenBusiness's Starter plan is $0 + state fees. However, it does not include Registered Agent service ($199/yr), an Operating Agreement ($99), or an EIN ($99).
Why is Northwest $39 while ZenBusiness is $0?
Northwest charges $39 upfront but includes 1 year of Registered Agent service ($125 value) and an Operating Agreement. ZenBusiness charges $0 upfront but charges $199/yr for Registered Agent service separately.
Which service is cheaper in Year 1?
If you only need barebones LLC filing and will act as your own registered agent, ZenBusiness ($0) is cheaper. If you need a Registered Agent and Operating Agreement, Northwest ($39 total) is significantly cheaper than ZenBusiness ($298+ total).
Which service offers better privacy?
Northwest Registered Agent is superior for privacy. They use their own address on state filings where permitted to keep your home address off public records.
