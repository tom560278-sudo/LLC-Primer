How to Start an LLC in Nebraska in 2026

Last reviewed: October 2026  ·  Primary sources: Nebraska Secretary of State, Nebraska Department of Revenue

Nebraska makes LLC formation relatively straightforward, but it has one requirement that makes the state different from most others: a domestic LLC must publish a notice of organization for three successive weeks in a qualifying legal newspaper near its designated office and then file proof of publication with the Nebraska Secretary of State.

Nebraska LLC: $100 Online Filing + a Real Publication Requirement
Certificate of Organization: $100 online | Publication: 3 successive weeks in a qualifying legal newspaper | Proof of Publication: $25 online | Biennial Report: Odd-numbered years ($25 online)

The biggest issue for Nebraska founders is not the Certificate of Organization itself. It is making sure the required publication is completed correctly and that proof is filed afterward.

Nebraska Revised Statute §21-193 requires a notice of organization to be published for three successive weeks in a legal newspaper of general circulation near the LLC’s designated office. The notice must contain the information required in the Certificate of Organization, and proof of publication must be filed with the Secretary of State.

Why Nebraska Is Different: Nebraska is not difficult to use for ordinary LLC formation, but founders need to budget for more than the state filing fee. A domestic Nebraska LLC generally has three formation-related costs:
1. Certificate of Organization: $100 online or $110 in writing
2. Newspaper publication: Cost varies by newspaper
3. Proof of Publication: $25 online or $30 in-office

The newspaper charge is not a fixed government fee. Each qualifying newspaper sets its own legal-notice pricing.

Nebraska LLC Publication Requirement

Nebraska Revised Statute §21-193 requires a notice of organization to be published for three successive weeks in a legal newspaper of general circulation near the LLC’s designated office. This requirement applies to a domestic Nebraska LLC. The statute also requires proof of publication to be filed with the Nebraska Secretary of State.

What the Nebraska Publication Rule Actually Requires
✓
Three successive weeks
The notice must run for three successive weeks in the qualifying newspaper.
✓
Qualifying legal newspaper
Publication must be in a legal newspaper of general circulation near the LLC’s designated office.
✓
Notice of organization
The notice must contain the information required under Nebraska’s Certificate of Organization rules.
✓
Proof filing
After publication, proof must be filed with the Nebraska Secretary of State ($25 online / $30 in-office).
Important 2026 Correction: There Is No Verified 45-Day Rule
Many online Nebraska LLC guides repeat an old or unsupported statement that the publication must be completed within 45 days. The current text of §21-193 does not establish that 45-day deadline. The statute says the notice must be published for three successive weeks and that proof of publication must be filed with the Secretary of State. It also provides a specific cure: if the required notice was not initially given but is subsequently published for the required period and proof is filed, the LLC’s acts before and after publication remain valid.

How to Complete Nebraska LLC Publication Correctly

1
Establish Your Nebraska Designated Office
A domestic Nebraska LLC must continuously maintain an office in Nebraska. Because the publication statute refers to a newspaper near the designated office, choose this address carefully.
2
Find a Qualifying Legal Newspaper
The newspaper must meet the statutory description of a legal newspaper of general circulation near the LLC’s designated office. Ask the newspaper: whether it qualifies, whether it serves the area, and how much the three-week publication will cost.
3
Submit the Notice
Provide the newspaper with accurate information from the LLC’s formation documents. Review the notice carefully before publication begins. A spelling error in the LLC’s legal name or an incorrect address can create unnecessary compliance problems.
4
Publish for Three Successive Weeks
Allow the newspaper to run the notice as required. Do not interrupt the publication cycle.
5
Obtain Proof of Publication
After the publication period is complete, obtain the newspaper’s affidavit or other proof of publication.
6
File Proof With the Secretary of State
The Nebraska Secretary of State currently lists the Affidavit/Proof of Publication filing at $25 online or $30 in-office. The newspaper’s publication charge is separate.

Choosing a Nebraska LLC Name

Before filing, choose a name that complies with Nebraska’s LLC naming requirements and is distinguishable from existing business names. Search the Nebraska Secretary of State’s business records before submitting your Certificate of Organization.

Nebraska LLC Registered Agent and Designated Office

Nebraska uses two related but separate concepts: designated office and agent for service of process. A domestic LLC must continuously maintain both in Nebraska. In a domestic Nebraska LLC, having a proper registered agent and designated office are distinct requirements.

RequirementDescription
Designated OfficeThe LLC must maintain an office in Nebraska. Does not have to be where the company conducts its day-to-day activities.
Agent for Service of ProcessMust be an individual who is a Nebraska resident, or another person authorized to transact business in Nebraska. A professional registered-agent company can serve this role.
Registered Agent vs. Designated Office: These addresses and roles should not automatically be treated as identical. A professional registered-agent service may provide the agent address, while the LLC’s designated office serves the separate statutory requirement.

Filing the Nebraska Certificate of Organization

A Nebraska domestic LLC is formed by filing a Certificate of Organization with the Nebraska Secretary of State. Information required includes: LLC name, initial designated office, initial agent for service of process, agent’s address, and professional-service information when applicable.

Nebraska LLC Operating Agreement

Nebraska does not require you to file an operating agreement with the Secretary of State. However, an operating agreement is an important internal document. It can establish: ownership percentages, member contributions, management authority, voting rights, profit and loss distributions, procedures for adding members, procedures when a member leaves, transfer restrictions, and dissolution procedures.

A single-member LLC still benefits from a written operating agreement as a written record showing how the company is structured. A multi-member LLC should generally have a detailed agreement addressing ownership, voting, distributions, management, and member exits.

Getting an EIN for Your Nebraska LLC

An Employer Identification Number (EIN) is a federal tax identification number issued by the IRS. The IRS provides EIN applications directly at no charge. For a newly formed Nebraska LLC, it is generally practical to obtain the EIN after the LLC has been officially formed. Do not pay a third party simply for access to the standard IRS EIN application.

Opening a Nebraska LLC Business Bank Account

A dedicated business bank account helps keep company transactions separate from personal finances. Banks commonly request: approved Certificate of Organization, EIN confirmation, operating agreement, government-issued identification, and ownership information.

Nebraska Biennial Report

Nebraska LLCs file a biennial report, not an annual report. The reporting cycle is based on odd-numbered years. The first biennial report is due between January 1 and April 1 of the odd-numbered year following the calendar year in which the LLC was formed. Subsequent reports are filed between January 1 and April 1 of each odd-numbered year.

Example: If your Nebraska LLC is formed in 2026, its first biennial report is due between January 1 and April 1, 2027.

Nebraska LLC Taxes

For federal tax purposes, a single-member LLC is generally treated as a disregarded entity unless it makes another election. A multi-member LLC is generally treated as a partnership unless it elects another classification. Understanding how LLCs are taxed can help you determine your federal and Nebraska obligations.

Nebraska has a state sales tax and local jurisdictions can impose additional sales taxes. Whether your LLC needs to register for sales tax depends on what you sell, where you sell it, and whether your activities create the applicable tax obligation. Nebraska-source business income may create state income-tax obligations. Nonresident owners can have additional Nebraska reporting or withholding requirements.

Foreign LLCs in Nebraska

If your LLC was formed in another state and you need authority to transact business in Nebraska, you generally use Nebraska’s foreign LLC registration process. The Nebraska Secretary of State currently lists the foreign LLC Certificate of Authority at $100 online + $10 certificate fee or $110 in-office + $10 certificate fee.

Do Not Confuse Domestic Formation With Foreign Qualification: A foreign LLC already exists under another state’s law. Registering it in Nebraska gives it authority to conduct business in Nebraska; it does not create a second Nebraska domestic LLC.
2026 BOI / FinCEN Update: Under FinCEN’s current rule, U.S.-created companies are exempt from federal Beneficial Ownership Information (BOI) reporting. That means a domestic Nebraska LLC is not required to file a BOI report. The rule became effective August 14, 2026. Do not rely on older BOI checklists that tell every newly formed U.S. LLC to file a FinCEN report.

Common Nebraska LLC Mistakes

Mistake 1: Assuming the $100 Filing Fee Is the Entire Formation Cost
Budget for: $100 state filing + newspaper cost + $25 proof filing = at minimum $125 + newspaper fees. Newspaper rates vary.
Mistake 2: Believing the Old 45-Day Publication Rule
Current §21-193 does not establish a 45-day deadline. The law requires three successive weeks of publication in a qualifying legal newspaper.
Mistake 3: Choosing the Newspaper Based Only on Price
Nebraska requires a qualifying legal newspaper of general circulation near the LLC’s designated office. Confirm qualifications before paying.
Mistake 4: Confusing the Registered Agent With the Designated Office
Nebraska requires both a designated office and an agent for service of process. These can be at different addresses.
Mistake 5: Forgetting the Proof Filing
After publication, obtain the affidavit from the newspaper and file it with the Secretary of State. This step is required.
Mistake 6: Treating a Biennial Report Like an Annual Report
Nebraska LLCs report in odd-numbered years, with a filing window of January 1 through April 1.
Bottom Line

Starting a Nebraska LLC involves a $100 online Certificate of Organization filing fee, plus a required newspaper publication for three successive weeks, and a $25 online proof-of-publication filing. The newspaper sets its own pricing, so the total formation cost varies. Nebraska also requires a biennial report in odd-numbered years ($25 online), filed between January 1 and April 1.

Nebraska LLC FAQ

1How much does it cost to form a Nebraska LLC?
At minimum: $100 online Certificate of Organization + newspaper publication cost (varies) + $25 proof-of-publication filing. Budget for at least $125 in government fees plus newspaper charges.
2Is there really a publication requirement for Nebraska LLCs?
Yes. Nebraska Revised Statute §21-193 requires a notice of organization to be published for three successive weeks in a qualifying legal newspaper near the LLC’s designated office. Proof of publication must be filed with the Secretary of State.
3Is there a 45-day deadline to complete Nebraska LLC publication?
The current text of §21-193 does not establish a general 45-day deadline. The statute requires three successive weeks of publication and filing of proof. It also provides a cure: if publication is later completed for the required period and proof is filed, the LLC’s acts remain valid.
4Does Nebraska require an annual report for LLCs?
No. Nebraska LLCs file a biennial report, not an annual report. The filing window is January 1 through April 1 of odd-numbered years. The fee is $25 electronic or $30 written.
5What is the difference between the designated office and the registered agent in Nebraska?
A domestic Nebraska LLC must maintain both a designated office in Nebraska and an agent for service of process. They are related but legally separate requirements that can be at different addresses.
6How much is the Nebraska LLC biennial report?
$25 for electronic filing or $30 for written filing. Due between January 1 and April 1 of each odd-numbered year.
7Does Nebraska require an operating agreement?
An operating agreement is not required to be filed with the Secretary of State. Creating one is recommended to document ownership, management authority, distributions, and other internal LLC rules.
8How much does newspaper publication cost in Nebraska?
Newspaper rates are not fixed by the state. Each qualifying legal newspaper sets its own legal-notice pricing. Contact the qualifying newspaper near your designated office for a quote before committing.
