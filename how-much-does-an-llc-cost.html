Mandatory LLC costs that can exist beyond the Articles filing

Evaluating an LLC state by its formation fee alone leads to expensive surprises. Several states enforce additional mandatory initial costs:

Alabama: mandatory name reservation fee

Alabama charges a $200 filing fee for Articles of Organization, but also requires a mandatory $28 Certificate of Name Reservation prior to filing, bringing the true formation fee to $228.

New York: the newspaper publication requirement

New York requires new LLCs to publish notice in two local newspapers designated by the county clerk for six consecutive weeks. Publication costs range from $300 to $1,500+ depending on your county, plus a $50 Certificate of Publication filing fee.

Nevada: mandatory initial list and state business license

While Nevada's Articles of Organization fee is $75, the state requires an Initial List ($150) and a State Business License ($200) filed concurrently, creating a mandatory initial government stack of $425.

How much does an EIN cost?

$0 when you obtain it directly from the IRS. Third-party formation services often charge $50–$100 to apply for an Employer Identification Number on your behalf, but applying directly on IRS.gov is free and instantaneous for US residents.

Can LLC formation and maintenance costs be tax-deductible?

Some startup costs can be deducted, but the old belief that "everything is a $5,000 startup deduction" is inaccurate. Federal tax treatment depends on the nature of the expense, when operations begin, and entity classification under IRS Section 195.

Startup costs vs. Organizational costs

IRS Publication 583 explains that a business can generally elect to deduct up to $5,000 of qualifying startup costs and up to $5,000 of qualifying organizational costs in the first year of active trade or business, reduced dollar-for-dollar when total costs exceed $50,000. Remaining amounts are amortized over 180 months.

The Out-of-State Formation Cost Trap

Forming in Wyoming or Delaware solely to save on state fees is often a costly mistake for local small businesses. If you operate your business in your home state (e.g., California or New York), your home state will require you to register as a Foreign LLC, forcing you to pay two set of state fees and two registered agent fees. Always form in the state where your physical business activities take place.

Five LLC cost mistakes to avoid

  • Comparing states only by the Articles filing fee: Mandatory initial extras like Nevada's $350 list/license or NY publication change the real initial stack.
  • Treating optional service fees as state costs: Registered agent services and formation service packages are optional private costs, not government taxes.
  • Ignoring recurring annual taxes: States like Delaware ($300 flat annual tax) or California ($800 annual tax) cost significantly more over 5 years.
  • Paying third parties for a free EIN: Applying directly on IRS.gov takes minutes and costs $0.
  • Falling into the foreign qualification trap: Out-of-state LLCs doing business locally must pay state fees in both states.
BUDGETING QUESTION
Which number should I compare first when choosing a state?
✓
Compare the mandatory Year 1 government stack and the recurring government stack separately. Then add only the conditional costs that match your facts and the private services you actually want. That produces a more defensible budget than ranking states by one filing fee.
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

LLC Cost FAQ

Government costs and state fees

01 What is the cheapest possible LLC formation fee in the current 2026 matrix?

The lowest government formation fee in the 2026 matrix is Kentucky ($40) or New Mexico ($50 online filing). However, total costs depend on mandatory recurring annual report fees.

02 Is the state filing fee the only mandatory cost?

No. Several states enforce mandatory extras at formation, such as Alabama's mandatory $28 name reservation, Nevada's $350 initial list and business license, or New York's $300–$1,500 newspaper publication requirement.

03 How much does a Nevada LLC cost to start?

A Nevada LLC costs $425 to start ($75 Articles of Organization + $150 Initial List of Managers + $200 State Business License), plus a recurring $350 annual fee.

04 Does every LLC have an annual fee?

No, a few states like New Mexico, Ohio, Missouri, and Arizona charge $0 in recurring annual state report fees. Most other states charge between $10 and $800 annually.

05 Is New Jersey's $150-per-owner partnership fee automatic for every multi-member LLC?

In New Jersey, entities classified as partnerships with 2 or more partners pay a $150-per-partner filing fee unless exempt based on gross income or specific active member criteria. Always review state tax rules.

Services, EIN and tax treatment

06 How much does LegalZoom charge to form an LLC in 2026?

LegalZoom's basic package starts at $0 + state fee, but registered agent service is an additional $299/year. Pro and Premium tiers range from $249 to $299 + state fee.

07 Does Northwest cost $39 per year as a registered agent?

Northwest charges $39 to form your LLC (which includes 1 year of registered agent service). After the first year, registered agent service renews at $125/year.

08 Is an EIN free?

Yes. An Employer Identification Number (EIN) is 100% free when applied for directly through the official IRS website (IRS.gov).

09 Can I deduct $5,000 of all LLC formation costs?

Under IRS Section 195, up to $5,000 in qualifying business startup costs and $5,000 in organizational costs can be deducted in year 1, provided total startup expenses don't exceed $50,000.

10 Should I form in the state with the lowest fee?

Not if you operate in another state. Operating a foreign LLC in your home state requires registering and paying state fees in both states, making it more expensive overall.
