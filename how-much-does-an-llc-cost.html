How Much Does an LLC Cost? (2026 50-State Fee Guide)

Last reviewed: September 21, 2026

The cost of an LLC is not one filing fee plus one annual fee. In 2026, a reliable budget has to separate formation filing, mandatory formation-stage extras, recurring entity filings, taxes or licenses, conditional costs, and optional private services.

That distinction changes the answer materially. A Nevada LLC has a $75 Articles fee but a $425 mandatory Initial state package. New York adds publication and a $50 Certificate of Publication. Nebraska adds publication and proof filing. California separates a $20 Statement of Information from its $800 annual tax. Tennessee's filing and annual-report fees depend on member count.

The six cost buckets that determine what an LLC really costs

This is also why a national "average annual LLC fee" is not very useful without a methodology. A biennial filing, Delaware's annual tax, Nevada's license, Tennessee's member-count fee and a state with no periodic report are not directly comparable numbers.

Mandatory LLC costs that can exist beyond the Articles filing

Alabama: the filing is not the only formation-stage requirement

Alabama currently charges $200 at the Secretary of State for a domestic LLC, but the state also requires a Certificate of Name Reservation before formation documents are filed. That makes "only the filing fee is mandatory" false as a national rule.

New York: publication can dominate the first-year budget

New York requires newly formed LLCs to publish notice in two local newspapers designated by the county clerk for six consecutive weeks. Publication costs range from $300 to $1,500+ depending on the county, plus a $50 state filing fee for the Certificate of Publication.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions About LLC Costs

01 Are LLC filing fees tax deductible?

Under IRS Section 195, startup costs up to $5,000 (including LLC formation fees and legal costs) can generally be deducted in your first year of business operations, subject to income thresholds.

02 Do I have to pay an annual fee for my LLC every year?

In most states, yes. Annual or biennial report fees range from $0 (like Ohio or New Mexico) to $300+ (like Delaware or California). Check your state's specific annual requirement to maintain good standing.

03 Is an EIN free from the IRS?

Yes! Applying for an EIN directly on IRS.gov is 100% free and issued immediately. You should never pay a third-party service just to obtain an EIN.
