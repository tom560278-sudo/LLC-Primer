doola combines company formation with registered-agent service, a U.S. business address, EIN assistance and additional bookkeeping, tax and business-management services. EIN & Non-US Founder Support

An EIN is issued by the IRS, and the application itself does not require paying the IRS a fee. International applicants who do not have a U.S. residence or principal place of business may use IRS telephone, fax, and mail options. doola includes EIN assistance with its Starter plan and specifically markets its service to founders establishing U.S. businesses from outside the country. Its current materials state that it handles the EIN process and provides guidance for opening a U.S. business bank account.

Northwest's core service is different. It can form an LLC and provide registered-agent and business-support services, but it does not bundle the same international banking and tax infrastructure into its basic formation package. For a U.S.-based founder who is comfortable obtaining an EIN directly from the IRS, paying specifically for EIN assistance may not be necessary.

⚠️ Formation providers do not replace individualized tax advice. EIN eligibility and federal tax obligations depend on the entity and the responsible party's circumstances.

Frequently Asked Questions

01 Is doola better than Northwest Registered Agent?
Neither is universally better. doola provides a broader combination of formation, EIN, registered-agent, U.S. address, banking guidance, bookkeeping and tax-related services. Northwest focuses more heavily on formation, registered-agent service, privacy-oriented business support and business identity tools.
02 How much does doola cost per year?
doola's current standard Starter price is $297 per year plus state fees. Its Tax & Compliance plan is $1,999/year and Business-in-a-Box™ is $2,999/year. Promotions may reduce the displayed price; verify the amount shown at checkout.
03 How much does Northwest Registered Agent cost?
Northwest currently advertises LLC formation for $39 plus state fees, with a first year of registered-agent service included when it forms the company. Registered-agent service is currently listed at $125 per year.
04 Can I get an EIN without a Social Security Number?
An EIN may be available to eligible international applicants without an SSN. The IRS provides alternative application procedures for applicants who cannot use the standard online process, including telephone, fax and mail options. doola provides EIN assistance as part of its Starter package.
05 Does doola work for U.S. residents?
Yes. doola's services are not limited to non-U.S. residents. The more relevant question is whether a U.S.-based founder needs the additional services included in doola's package.
06 Does Northwest work for non-U.S. founders?
Non-U.S. founders should not assume that Northwest is automatically unavailable. However, Northwest's core offering does not bundle the same international banking, EIN coordination and tax/back-office services that doola emphasizes.
07 What is Privacy by Default® at Northwest?
Privacy by Default® refers to Northwest's approach of using its business address where legally permitted rather than automatically placing a customer's home address in the registered-agent role. It can reduce the public exposure of a home address, but it does not guarantee that a personal address will be absent from every government or business record.
08 Does doola include a registered agent?
Yes. doola's current Starter plan includes registered-agent service, along with formation, EIN assistance and a U.S. business address.
09 Does Northwest include a registered agent?
Yes. Northwest includes the first year of registered-agent service when it forms the company. Its current standalone registered-agent price is $125 per year.
10 Is an EIN free?
The IRS does not charge a fee to issue an EIN. A formation company may charge for assistance with the application, but the federal EIN itself is issued by the IRS without a government application fee.
11 Which service is more appropriate for a non-U.S. founder?
Start by identifying what support you actually need. If you need formation plus EIN assistance, a U.S. business address and bank-account guidance, doola's Starter package is specifically structured around those services. If you only need formation and registered-agent service, compare Northwest and other providers based on current eligibility, pricing and the services available for your situation.
12 doola vs Northwest for ongoing costs?
Northwest has a lower RA renewal rate at $125/year versus doola's $297/year on the Starter plan. However, doola includes more bundled services. The right choice depends on which services you actually need.
13 Can I switch providers later?
Yes. You can change your registered agent or formation provider at any time by following the applicable state procedure.
14 Are state filing fees included?
No. The advertised formation prices from both companies are separate from the state's LLC filing fee.
