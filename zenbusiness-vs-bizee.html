ZenBusiness and Bizee (formerly Incfile, rebranded in 2023) both offer LLC formation starting at $0 plus state filing fees. The main differences are plan structure, registered-agent service, compliance features, and optional business tools. ZenBusiness places more of its compliance and formation features inside its Pro and Premium subscriptions, while Bizee includes first-year registered-agent coverage with its formation packages.

This comparison looks at pricing, included services, registered-agent costs, processing, support, and renewal considerations so you can decide which structure fits your LLC formation needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

01 Is Bizee the same as Incfile?
Yes. Bizee is the current brand associated with the business previously known as Incfile. The company rebranded in 2023 and continues to provide business formation and related services.
02 Which is cheaper: ZenBusiness or Bizee?
Both have a $0 starting formation service fee plus the state's filing fee. Bizee currently includes the first year of registered-agent service with new formations, while ZenBusiness charges separately for registered-agent service.
03 How much does ZenBusiness cost?
ZenBusiness currently lists Starter at $0 plus state fees, Pro at $199 plus state fees, and Premium at $299 plus state fees. Registered-agent service is a separate $199/year add-on.
04 How much does Bizee cost?
Bizee currently lists Basic at $0 plus state fee, Standard at $199 plus state fee, and Premium at $299 plus state fee. The formation package is described as a one-time service fee.
05 Does Bizee include a registered agent?
Yes. Bizee currently states that new LLC formation packages include one free year of registered-agent service. Its standalone registered-agent service is listed at $149/year.
06 Does ZenBusiness include a registered agent?
Not in standard formation packages. ZenBusiness currently lists registered-agent service separately at $199/year.
07 Which company has faster filing?
ZenBusiness: 7–10 days Starter, 1 day Pro/Premium. Bizee: approximately two weeks Standard, approximately four business days Premium. State processing is separate from both providers' times.
08 Are there additional costs after forming an LLC?
Yes. State annual reports, franchise taxes, business licenses, provider subscriptions, and registered-agent services can all create ongoing costs.
09 Do I need an operating agreement?
An operating agreement is generally an internal LLC document rather than a state filing. Even where it is not required, it can document ownership, management, voting, and distributions.
10 Do I need an EIN?
Not every LLC needs one. Multi-member LLCs, employers, and certain tax elections commonly require an EIN. The IRS issues EINs directly at no charge.
11 Is there a better alternative to ZenBusiness and Bizee?
Several other providers exist, including Northwest Registered Agent. The appropriate provider depends on the services you need, the state where you are forming, privacy considerations, and ongoing costs.
12 Which one should I choose?
Start with the service you need. If compliance tools are a priority, compare ZenBusiness's plans. If first-year RA coverage and a one-time formation fee matter, compare Bizee's plans.
