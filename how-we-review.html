How We Review & Compare Services

Our 6-Pillar Evaluation Framework

When evaluating commercial formation platforms (such as Northwest Registered Agent, ZenBusiness, LegalZoom, Bizee, Tailor Brands, and doola), we assess providers against six practical criteria:

1. True Total Cost & Renewal Transparency

Many formation companies advertise promotional "$0 + state fee" packages that conceal expensive mandatory subscription renewals or recurring compliance fees. We analyze:

  • First-Year vs. Ongoing Costs: Does the advertised price include registered agent service, or does it trigger an expensive renewal after year one?
  • Upsell Aggressiveness: How many checkout add-ons (such as EIN filing fees for a free IRS document, generic operating agreements, or unnecessary banking services) are pre-selected during checkout?
  • Cancellation Ease: Can users cancel recurring registered agent or compliance subscriptions online without hidden retention barriers?

2. Registered Agent Quality & Privacy Protection

A registered agent must maintain a physical office in your state to receive service of process and state correspondence. We evaluate:

  • Address Privacy: Does the provider allow you to use their commercial street address on public state documents to protect your personal home address?
  • Document Scanning & Forwarding: How quickly are official legal summons and state tax notices digitized and uploaded to your client portal?
  • Local In-House Offices vs. Third-Party Contracting: Does the company own and staff its own offices in all 50 states (e.g., Northwest), or do they subcontract to unrelated regional agents?

3. Turnaround Time & Processing Options

State filing speeds depend heavily on Secretary of State processing queues, but formation services differ in how quickly they prepare and transmit documents. We check whether providers charge inflated internal rush fees on top of statutory state expedite fees.

4. Operating Agreement & EIN Transparency

An EIN is 100% free when obtained directly from the IRS website. We look favorably on companies that provide free customizable operating agreement templates and clarify that founders can acquire an EIN directly from the government without third-party surcharges.

5. Customer Support & Domain Expertise

We review the availability and expertise of each service's customer support. Providers offering experienced, in-house business support teams with state-specific entity filing knowledge score higher than platforms that rely on automated scripts or outsourced third-party ticketing queues.

6. International & Non-Resident Compatibility

For international founders forming U.S. LLCs without a Social Security Number (SSN), we assess specialized providers (such as doola and Northwest) based on their ability to handle Form SS-4 fax applications, remote business bank account introductions, and registered agent representation.
