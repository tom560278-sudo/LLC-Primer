How We Review & Compare Services

Transparent Methodology: LLC Primer reviews rely on publicly available provider pricing pages, terms of service, plan inclusions and exclusions, renewal pricing, and official government documentation where applicable. We compare these sources to explain advertised costs, service features, and limitations. We do not claim to conduct hands-on checkout testing, mystery shopping, or customer support testing unless those activities have actually been performed and documented.

Our 6-Pillar Evaluation Framework

When evaluating commercial formation platforms (such as Northwest Registered Agent, ZenBusiness, LegalZoom, Bizee, Tailor Brands, and doola), we assess providers based on publicly available documentation across six objective criteria:

1. True Total Cost & Renewal Transparency

Commercial formation platforms frequently advertise low introductory or promotional "$0 + state fee" packages. We examine published pricing tables and official terms of service to evaluate the true ongoing cost of each service:

  • First-Year vs. Ongoing Costs: We compare publicly advertised first-year prices, registered agent inclusions, renewal rates, and recurring fees where disclosed.
  • Optional Add-Ons & Cancellation Terms: We review published plan details, optional add-on prices, and available cancellation policies. We do not claim to have tested checkout defaults or cancellation processes unless documented testing has been performed.

2. Registered Agent Terms & Privacy Protection

A registered agent must maintain a physical office in your state of formation to receive legal service of process and official state notices during normal business hours. We review documented service features:

  • Address Privacy: We review published registered agent services and explain how providers describe the use of their addresses on public records.
  • Document Scanning & Forwarding: We compare the document-handling services and processing commitments providers publicly describe. We do not infer actual processing speed without supporting evidence.
  • Office Network & Third-Party Agents: We review available provider documentation about their office networks and use of third-party agents. We do not assume ownership or staffing arrangements without verification.

3. Turnaround Estimates & Processing Disclosures

Formation processing timelines are determined primarily by official Secretary of State workload and statutory processing queues. We compare published service fees and advertised processing options with applicable state expedite fees where official information is available. We distinguish provider estimates from official state processing times and do not describe fees as inflated without a clear, documented basis for comparison.

4. Operating Agreement & EIN Transparency

An Employer Identification Number (EIN) is issued directly by the IRS at zero cost. We review provider package inclusions against official IRS procedures, evaluating whether platforms offer standard customizable operating agreement templates and accurately inform users that an EIN can be obtained directly from the IRS website for free.

5. Customer Support Channels & Stated Availability

We compare publicly listed customer support channels, published service hours, help centers, and provider documentation where available. We describe advertised support options without implying that response times, staff expertise, or service quality have been independently tested unless documented evidence supports those conclusions.

6. International & Non-Resident Feature Availability

For international founders forming U.S. LLCs, we review providers' published information about non-resident eligibility, EIN application assistance, registered agent services, and business banking introductions where applicable. We distinguish advertised services from independently verified eligibility and outcomes, and we do not guarantee bank account approval or a particular application result.
