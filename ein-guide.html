Obtain Federal EIN (SS-4 Tax ID)

Official 2026 IRS Application Requirements & Step-by-Step Guide

LLC Editorial Team • Verified for 2026 • Last reviewed: September 7, 2026

An EIN (Employer Identification Number) is a nine-digit federal tax identification number issued by the IRS. It is free to obtain directly from the IRS, but an EIN is not automatically required for every LLC. A multi-member LLC, an LLC with employees, and an LLC using certain corporate or excise-tax treatments generally need one. By contrast, a single-member LLC with no employees and no relevant excise-tax liability may not need a separate EIN for federal income-tax purposes.

That distinction matters because an EIN is often described as a business version of a Social Security number. The analogy is convenient, but it can hide important rules. For example, a disregarded single-member LLC may have its own EIN for banking or employment-tax purposes while still using the owner's taxpayer identification number for certain federal income-tax reporting.

This guide focuses on the current IRS process: who actually needs an EIN, who can use the online assistant, what changes for international founders, which address information the IRS asks for, and what to save after the number is issued. Does Your LLC Actually Need an EIN?

The answer depends on federal tax treatment and what the business does. An EIN is mandatory in several common situations, but the IRS also recognizes a narrower case in which a single-member LLC can operate for federal income-tax purposes without a separate EIN.

When an LLC needs a separate EIN
✓
Two or more members
A domestic multi-member LLC is generally treated as a partnership unless it elects corporate classification. Partnerships need an EIN.
✓
Employees
A disregarded single-member LLC uses its own name and EIN for federal employment-tax reporting and payments.
✓
Corporation or S corporation tax treatment
An LLC taxed as a corporation needs an EIN for the entity's federal filings.
✓
Certain excise-tax obligations
A single-member disregarded LLC may need its own EIN when it must file specified excise-tax forms.
-
Single-member LLC with no employees or relevant excise tax
A separate EIN is generally not required for federal income-tax purposes, although a bank or state rule may still make obtaining one useful.
❓ Question that repeatedly comes up among new LLC owners:

"I am the only owner and I have no employees. Do I still need an EIN?"

✓
For federal income-tax purposes, not necessarily. The IRS says a single-member disregarded LLC with no employees and no relevant excise-tax liability does not need a separate EIN. However, the LLC may still obtain one when a bank or state requirement calls for it.
The key is to separate the IRS requirement from a bank's onboarding policy. They are not the same rule.

Can You Use the IRS Online EIN Tool?

The online assistant is the fastest route when you qualify. The IRS can issue the number immediately after it validates and approves the application. Still, the online tool is not available to every applicant.

Online EIN Assistant eligibility
✓
The organization is domestic
It was formed or created in the United States or a U.S. territory.
✓
The principal place of business is in the U.S. or a U.S. territory
If the principal business is outside the United States, the online assistant is not the correct method.
✓
The responsible party can be identified
The online process requires the responsible party's valid SSN or ITIN.
✕
The principal place of business is outside the U.S.
International applicants in that situation use the telephone, fax, or mail procedures instead.
💡 Online availability now includes weekend hours:
Currently, the IRS lists the online tool as available Monday–Friday from 6:00 a.m. to 1:00 a.m. the next day Eastern, Saturday from 6:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., and Sunday from 7:00 p.m. to midnight. Availability can change, so use the IRS page above as the final check before applying.

What Information Should You Prepare Before Applying?

You do not need a large document package to complete the federal application, but accuracy matters. In particular, the responsible-party and address fields should reflect what the IRS is actually asking for rather than whatever wording appears on a formation-service dashboard.

INFORMATION WHAT TO HAVE READY WHY IT MATTERS
Legal entity name The LLC name as formed under state law Inconsistent names can create processing and matching problems.
Trade name DBA or trade name, if different Form SS-4 separates the legal name from the trade name.
Responsible party The individual who ultimately owns or controls the entity Nominees are not supposed to apply as the responsible party.
SSN or ITIN Responsible party's number when using the online tool The online assistant requires it for ordinary non-government applicants.
Mailing and physical location Where IRS correspondence goes and, if different, the actual street location SS-4 treats mailing and street address as separate fields.
Entity and activity details Member count, reason for applying, principal activity and employee information These answers help establish the federal tax account correctly.

If you are forming an LLC, the IRS now tells applicants to form the entity with the state before requesting the EIN. Applying first is better described as a potential source of processing delay, rather than an automatic rejection in every case.

How the Online EIN Application Works

The screens and wording of the IRS assistant can change, so a reliable guide should explain the decisions instead of pretending the interface will always contain the same eight buttons. The current process follows this general sequence.

BEFORE THE SESSION
Form the LLC and gather the responsible-party information
Have the legal name, addresses, member count, principal activity and responsible party's SSN or ITIN ready.
START
Open the EIN Assistant from IRS.gov
Use the IRS domain rather than an advertisement or a site that mimics a government application.
ENTITY QUESTIONS
Describe the LLC and why the EIN is being requested
The answers should match the business that actually exists, including the number of members and the reason for applying.
IDENTITY
Identify the responsible party
For ordinary applicants, the responsible party is an individual who ultimately owns or controls the entity — not a nominee used only to file paperwork.
REVIEW
Check the legal name, addresses and activity before submission
Because the session expires after 15 minutes of inactivity, prepare first and review carefully before submitting.
IF APPROVED
Save the EIN assignment notice
The online system lets taxpayers view, print and save the assignment notice (CP 575) at the end of the session. Keep a secure copy with the LLC's permanent records.

Special Rules & Considerations

There Is a Daily Limit

The IRS limits issuance to one EIN per responsible party per day, regardless of whether the request is made online, by phone, fax, or mail. Therefore, submitting duplicates after an error is not a good troubleshooting strategy. First confirm what happened with the original application.

Mailing Address and Physical Address Are Different Fields

Form SS-4 distinguishes the address used for correspondence from the entity's street location. Lines 4a–4b are for the mailing address, and that mailing address may include a P.O. box. Lines 5a–5b ask for a physical street address only when it differs from the mailing address, and the IRS instructions say not to put a P.O. box in the street-address field.

This distinction is more useful than a blanket statement that "a virtual address works for an EIN." Some services provide only mail receipt, while others provide different address arrangements. What matters on Form SS-4 is answering the mailing and physical-location questions accurately. If the address later changes, the IRS uses Form 8822-B for business address and responsible-party updates.

Getting an EIN When the Principal Business Is Outside the U.S.

An international founder can obtain an EIN even when the responsible party does not have an SSN or ITIN. However, the application route changes when there is no U.S. legal residence, principal place of business, principal office, or agency.

ROUTE CURRENT IRS PROCEDURE TIMING / AVAILABILITY
Phone 267-941-1099 for international applicants; caller must be authorized to receive the EIN and answer SS-4 questions Monday–Friday, 6:00 a.m.–11:00 p.m. Eastern
Fax Form SS-4 to 855-215-1627 from within the U.S. or 304-707-9471 from outside the U.S. Fax service is available 24/7; IRS timing can vary
Mail Form SS-4 to IRS, Attn: EIN International Operation, Cincinnati, OH 45999 IRS says to allow roughly 4 weeks

A 2026 W-9 Rule That Single-Member LLC Owners Often Miss

💡 Your LLC EIN is not automatically the TIN you put on Form W-9:
For a single-member LLC that is disregarded for federal income-tax purposes, current Form W-9 instructions say to furnish the owner's TIN, not the disregarded LLC's EIN, for payee identification. An LLC can therefore have an EIN for one purpose while federal income-tax information reporting still follows the owner's taxpayer identity.

What to Do After the EIN Is Issued

First, save the assignment notice (CP 575) in more than one secure location and record which entity the number belongs to. An EIN should not be casually reused across newly created entities just because the ownership is similar.

Next, check the requirements of the bank you actually want to use. An EIN is commonly requested for LLC account opening, along with your approved Articles of Organization and Operating Agreement. If you lose your original assignment documentation, follow the IRS verification process for a 147C verification letter.

Finally, continue through the post-formation sequence. See the business setup checklist to complete banking, licenses, insurance, and compliance in order.

EIN Mistakes Worth Avoiding

Treating "Free" as "Everyone Must Do It"

The IRS charges nothing for an EIN, but that does not make a separate EIN federally mandatory for every disregarded single-member LLC. Check the actual requirement first, then obtain one if your tax situation, bank, or state calls for it.

Using a Nominee as the Responsible Party

The responsible party is supposed to be the individual who ultimately owns or controls the entity. The IRS specifically warns that nominees are not authorized to apply as the responsible party.

Assuming an Application Before State Formation Is Automatically Rejected

Form the LLC with the state first. Current IRS guidance says applying before the entity is formed may delay processing.

Submitting Another Application Because You Lost the Confirmation

A lost notice is a records problem, not a reason to create a second tax identity. Recover or verify the existing EIN instead. Duplicate numbers can complicate bank records and tax filings.

Using the Online Assistant When the Business Is International

If the principal place of business is outside the U.S., use the IRS international routes. The online assistant has a specific domestic eligibility test.

Frequently Asked Questions About EINs

Requirement and Online Application

Is an EIN free?
Yes. The IRS issues Employer Identification Numbers completely free of charge. You should never pay a third party or website to file a standard IRS EIN application unless you are hiring a professional service to handle broader setup tasks.
How fast can I get an EIN online?
Immediately. If you complete the online EIN Assistant on IRS.gov and your application passes system validation, your EIN is generated instantly and presented in an official assignment notice (CP 575 PDF).
Can a single-member LLC get an EIN even if the IRS does not require one?
Yes. Single-member LLCs commonly request an EIN to open a business bank account, avoid sharing their personal SSN on business contracts, or prepare for future hiring.

International, Address and Records Questions

Can I get an EIN without an SSN or ITIN?
Yes. International founders without an SSN or ITIN can apply by calling the IRS international line (+1 267-941-1099) or by submitting Form SS-4 by fax or mail. You write "Foreign" or "N/A" in line 7b.
Can I use a P.O. box on Form SS-4?
You can use a P.O. box for the mailing address (Lines 4a-4b), but not for the physical street address location (Lines 5a-5b).
Does an EIN expire?
No. Once an EIN has been assigned to an entity, it remains the permanent federal tax identification number for that business and is never re-assigned to another company.
