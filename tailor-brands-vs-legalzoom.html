Tailor Brands combines LLC formation with business-building and branding tools. LegalZoom approaches LLC formation from a broader legal-services perspective. The relevant question is what you want beyond the basic state filing: branding and business-building tools, or broader legal-service options?

Neither provider is universally the right choice. Founded in 2014, Tailor Brands focuses on formation plus brand identity. Founded in 2001, LegalZoom provides formation combined with attorney access, legal documents, and a broad legal-service platform.

Key Differences

Core Difference

Tailor Brands is a business-building platform with LLC formation. LegalZoom is a broader legal and business-services platform that also handles LLC formation. Tailor Brands began as a branding-focused technology company and has expanded into business formation and management. LegalZoom has operated in the online legal-services market for more than two decades.

Where They Overlap

  • LLC formation and state filing support
  • Name-check services
  • Optional EIN filing
  • Operating agreement services
  • Compliance-related services
  • Online account management
Key Insight: If your main goal is simply to create an LLC at the lowest possible service cost, do not assume either company's paid tier is necessary. Compare the basic plans and consider whether you actually need RA, paid EIN filing, OA service, branding tools, or attorney access.

Branding — Tailor Brands' Main Differentiator

Tailor Brands' current Elite plan includes online business and branding tools: a DIY website/online store, one year of domain registration, logo tools, a digital business card, social-media tools, and business-card tools. This can be useful if you want your formation provider and initial branding workflow in one dashboard.

Legal Services — LegalZoom's Main Differentiator

LegalZoom's primary distinction is the breadth of legal and business services available around the formation: attorney access through qualifying plans, customizable legal documents, e-signatures, operating agreements, EIN services, trademarks, and other business-management products. Attorney access is not a substitute for choosing an attorney independently when you need advice specific to your business or legal situation.

Frequently Asked Questions

01 Is Tailor Brands better than LegalZoom for LLC formation?
There is no universal answer. Tailor Brands is more focused on combining LLC formation with branding and business-building tools. LegalZoom provides a broader legal-services platform, including attorney access and legal documents on qualifying plans.
02 What is the starting price for Tailor Brands?
Tailor Brands currently advertises LLC formation starting at $0 + state fees on its Lite plan. Essential is $199/year and Elite is $249/year, also plus state fees.
03 What is the starting price for LegalZoom?
LegalZoom currently advertises LLC formation starting at $0 + state filing fees. Its current paid formation packages are Pro at $249 and Premium at $299, plus state fees.
04 Does Tailor Brands include a registered agent?
No. Tailor Brands' registered-agent service is separate from its formation plans. Verify the current price and purchase requirements during checkout.
05 How much does LegalZoom's registered agent cost?
LegalZoom currently lists registered-agent service at $249/year, with automatic renewal unless canceled.
06 Does Tailor Brands include an EIN?
EIN filing is a paid service rather than a free feature of the formation plan. You can also apply directly through the IRS at no charge if you are eligible.
07 Does LegalZoom include an EIN?
LegalZoom currently lists EIN service at $79 as a standalone business service, while qualifying paid formation packages include the service.
08 Does Tailor Brands include an operating agreement?
The current Tailor Brands pricing page lists establishing an operating agreement as part of the Essential and Elite plans. The Lite plan does not list it as an included feature.
09 Does LegalZoom include an operating agreement?
LegalZoom currently lists an operating agreement at $99 as a standalone service. Its qualifying paid formation plans include an operating agreement.
10 Which service is better for an online business?
If you specifically want LLC formation plus an integrated website, domain, logo, and social-media tools, Tailor Brands Elite is more directly relevant. If your online business is more likely to need attorney access, contracts, or trademarks, LegalZoom may be more relevant.
11 Does Tailor Brands charge recurring fees?
Yes. Essential and Elite are currently advertised as annual plans at $199 and $249 respectively. Tailor Brands also states that the bookkeeping and invoicing trial converts to $39/month after 30 days unless canceled.
12 Does LegalZoom charge recurring fees?
Some LegalZoom services are recurring. Its registered-agent service is currently $249/year, and attorney or other subscription services can renew after an included trial period.
13 Are state filing fees included?
No. The advertised formation prices from both companies are separate from the state's LLC filing fee.
14 Can I get an LLC without paying either company for an EIN?
Yes. An eligible applicant can generally apply for an EIN directly through the IRS without paying a formation company to submit the application.
15 Do I need a commercial registered agent?
Not necessarily. State rules determine who can serve as a registered agent and what address and availability requirements apply. In many situations, an eligible owner or other individual can serve as the registered agent.
