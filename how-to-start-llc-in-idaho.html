How to Start an LLC in Idaho in 2026

Last reviewed: September 2026  ·  Primary sources: Idaho Secretary of State (SOSBiz), Idaho State Tax Commission

Starting an LLC in Idaho involves choosing an available business name, appointing an Idaho registered agent, filing a Certificate of Organization with the Idaho Secretary of State, and keeping the business in good standing through its required filings. For most new Idaho LLCs, the state filing fee is $100 when filed online through SOSBiz. Paper filings are subject to an additional $20 manual-processing fee. Idaho also requires LLCs to file an annual report, but the state does not charge a filing fee for the annual report.

How to Start an LLC in Idaho: 5 Steps

1
Choose and Search Your Idaho LLC Name

Your LLC name must include an accepted LLC designator and be distinguishable from existing registered business names. The Secretary of State recommends checking name availability before filing through SOSBiz. Idaho allows a business name to be reserved for four months for a base fee of $20. A name reservation is optional.

2
Choose an Idaho Registered Agent

Every Idaho LLC must maintain a registered agent with a physical street address in Idaho. The registered agent can be an individual Idaho resident or a business entity authorized to do business in Idaho. A P.O. Box cannot be used as the registered office address, and the registered agent must be available during normal business hours.

A commercial agent can be useful for owners who do not have an Idaho address or who prefer to separate their personal address from the company’s registered-agent record where permitted.

3
File the Certificate of Organization

Idaho uses a Certificate of Organization to create a domestic LLC. The standard online filing fee is $100. Paper filing adds a $20 manual-processing fee (total $120). File electronically through SOSBiz. The filing includes: LLC name, principal office address, registered agent and registered office, at least one governor, mailing address, and organizer information and signature.

4
Create an Operating Agreement

Idaho LLCs operate under an agreement between the owners, commonly called an operating agreement. The Secretary of State does not require it to be filed. An operating agreement can establish: ownership percentages, member contributions, management responsibilities, voting rights, distribution of profits, adding or removing members, transfers of ownership, and dissolution procedures.

5
Get an EIN and Set Up Your Business

After the LLC is approved, complete the normal post-formation tasks: apply for an EIN from the IRS (free) when required, open a dedicated business bank account, keep business and personal finances separate, create and maintain your operating agreement, check federal/state/county/city licensing requirements, track Idaho annual-report deadlines, and register for applicable tax accounts.

Idaho Registered Agent Requirements

An Idaho LLC must have a registered agent located in Idaho to receive official documents and legal notices on behalf of the LLC. Idaho requires the registered agent to maintain a physical street address in the state.

SOSBiz — Idaho’s Online Business Filing System

SOSBiz is the Idaho Secretary of State’s online system for registering and managing businesses. Through SOSBiz, business owners can: register a new LLC, search Idaho businesses, file annual reports, file amendments, access business records, request certificates of existence, and manage other business filings. An SOSBiz account is required to file business forms and annual reports online.

Idaho LLC Taxes

Forming an LLC does not by itself determine how the business is taxed. For federal purposes, an LLC may receive different tax treatment depending on whether it has one member, multiple members, or makes an election to be taxed as a corporation.

Idaho individual income tax5.3% rate for 2025 taxable income
Idaho business income tax5.3% rate for 2025; may apply depending on entity and activity
Sales taxMay apply to taxable sales in Idaho

Idaho Tax Commission Note: A business generally must file an Idaho income-tax return when it is transacting business in Idaho, registered to do business in Idaho, or has income attributable to Idaho. Because LLC tax treatment can vary significantly, use current IRS and Idaho Tax Commission guidance for your specific structure.

Idaho LLC Annual Report

Idaho LLCs must file an annual report with the Secretary of State. Unlike many states, Idaho does not charge a state filing fee for the annual report. Annual reports can be filed online through SOSBiz.

🚨 Watch Out for Third-Party Mailings: Be cautious of third-party mailings that ask you to pay a private company to file an Idaho annual report. The Idaho Secretary of State has specifically warned about solicitations that make private filing services appear necessary when the state filing itself can be completed directly through SOSBiz at no charge.

Foreign LLCs Doing Business in Idaho

If an LLC was formed in another state and will conduct business in Idaho, it may need to register as a foreign business entity with the Idaho Secretary of State. Idaho requires a Foreign Registration Statement before conducting business in the state. The state also requires a Certificate of Existence or Good Standing from the home state dated within 90 days of filing. Current fee: $100 base fee (paper adds the $20 manual-processing fee).

How to Dissolve an Idaho LLC

If you decide to permanently close an Idaho LLC, formally dissolving the entity is generally preferable to simply stopping business activity. The Idaho Secretary of State provides a Statement of Dissolution for LLCs. Before closing, consider: completing outstanding contracts, paying business debts, collecting outstanding receivables, closing business bank accounts, cancelling applicable licenses and registrations, handling final federal and state tax filings, and maintaining required business records.

Frequently Asked Questions

1How much does it cost to start an LLC in Idaho?
The standard online Certificate of Organization fee is $100. Paper filings have an additional $20 manual-processing fee, making the paper total $120. Name reservation ($20), registered-agent services, licenses, permits, and other optional or business-specific expenses can add to the total.
2Does Idaho charge an annual LLC fee?
Idaho requires LLCs to file an annual report, but the Secretary of State currently lists no state filing fee for the annual report.
3How long does it take to form an LLC in Idaho?
The Idaho Secretary of State currently says business filings are being processed approximately 7–10 business days from the filing date. Processing times can change with filing volume. Expedited service is available for certain filings (same-day if received by 1 p.m. Mountain Time).
4Can I be my own registered agent in Idaho?
Yes, if you meet Idaho’s requirements. You need a physical street address in Idaho and must be available during normal business hours. A P.O. Box cannot be used as the registered office address.
5Does an Idaho LLC need an operating agreement?
Idaho’s Secretary of State does not require the operating agreement to be filed with the state. An operating agreement can nevertheless be useful for documenting ownership, management, voting, distributions, and other internal rules.
6Do Idaho LLCs pay state income tax?
Idaho has both an individual income tax and a business income tax system. The state’s published tax guidance currently lists a 5.3% rate for 2025 taxable income. Exact tax treatment depends on the LLC’s federal classification, owners, income, and activities.
7Can I reserve an Idaho LLC name?
Yes. Idaho allows a legal-entity name to be reserved for four months. The current base reservation fee is $20.
8Can a non-U.S. resident form an Idaho LLC?
A person does not necessarily need to live in Idaho to own an Idaho LLC, but the company still needs an eligible Idaho registered agent with a physical Idaho street address. Non-U.S. owners should also review federal tax, EIN, banking, and applicable state requirements before forming the company.
9Does Idaho require a business license for an LLC?
Forming an LLC is not the same as obtaining a business license. Licensing requirements depend on the business activity, location, industry, and applicable state or local rules. Check Idaho’s state agencies and your local city or county.
10How do I dissolve an Idaho LLC?
Idaho LLCs can be formally dissolved by filing the appropriate Statement of Dissolution with the Secretary of State (current fee: $20 manual-processing charge for paper). Before dissolution, handle outstanding debts, contracts, taxes, licenses, and business records.
