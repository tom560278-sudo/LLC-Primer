About LLC Primer

LLC Primer is an educational publishing resource dedicated to helping entrepreneurs, founders, and small business owners understand how limited liability companies (LLCs) operate across all 50 U.S. states and the District of Columbia. We provide accurate, plain-language guides covering LLC formation, state filing costs, taxation structures, registered agent statutory requirements, and ongoing compliance obligations.

Our Mission & Methodology

Our goal is to provide accurate, unbiased, and transparent business formation guidance — completely free of confusing legal jargon, hidden commercial traps, or unverified claims. We continuously research state corporate division filing requirements, fee schedules, statutory publication mandates, and tax deadlines directly from primary government authorities:

  • Official state filing divisions via the National Association of Secretaries of State (NASS)
  • Federal tax classifications and EIN rules from the Internal Revenue Service (IRS)
  • Small business formation and capitalization guidance from the U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA)
  • Self-employment earnings and payroll thresholds administered by the Social Security Administration (SSA)

What We Cover

  • Step-by-step formation walkthroughs for all 50 U.S. states
  • Accurate state formation filing fees, annual/biennial report schedules, and state tax baselines
  • Structural comparisons: LLC vs. Sole Proprietorship, S-Corporation election, and C-Corporation structures
  • Internal company documents, including Operating Agreements and Member Control Agreements
  • Statutory Registered Agent requirements, commercial provider reviews, and self-agent privacy implications
  • Non-resident U.S. LLC considerations: remote banking, ITIN/EIN acquisition, and federal reporting

Commitment to Source-Based Accuracy

LLC Primer is maintained by an independent editorial research team dedicated to providing accurate, objective educational guidance. We do not use unverified third-party content or automated scraping; every statutory filing fee, state deadline, and regulatory requirement is researched and cross-checked directly against official state corporate divisions and primary government records.

We adhere to strict editorial independence. Commercial relationships never influence our factual assessments, provider evaluations, or fee reporting. To review our complete publishing principles and testing methodologies, visit our dedicated Editorial Policy and How We Review pages.
