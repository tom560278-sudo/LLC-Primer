How to Start an LLC in Kentucky in 2026

Last reviewed: September 21, 2026  ·  Primary sources: Kentucky Secretary of State, Kentucky Revised Statutes, Kentucky Department of Revenue, FinCEN

To start an LLC in Kentucky in 2026, choose a compliant LLC name, appoint a Kentucky registered agent, file the Articles of Organization with the Kentucky Secretary of State, and complete the required county-clerk filing. The Kentucky Secretary of State filing fee is $40. Kentucky also requires an exact or conformed copy of the Articles of Organization to be filed with the county clerk in the county where the LLC maintains its registered office.

After formation, Kentucky LLCs generally need to file an annual report with the Secretary of State by June 30. The annual report fee is $15. Businesses subject to Kentucky’s Limited Liability Entity Tax (LLET) may also have a separate Department of Revenue filing and tax obligation.

⚠
Keep Kentucky’s Two Recurring Compliance Systems Separate: The annual report is filed with the Kentucky Secretary of State and is due by June 30. The LLET is administered by the Kentucky Department of Revenue and is a separate tax obligation. They are different filings handled by different agencies.

Kentucky LLC Formation Roadmap

1
Confirm the LLC Name
Search Kentucky’s business records before filing. The LLC’s legal name must satisfy Kentucky’s naming and distinguishability requirements. Name reservation is optional ($15 for 120 days).
2
Choose a Kentucky Registered Agent
The LLC must maintain a registered agent and registered office in Kentucky. The registered agent receives service of process and official communications for the company.
3
File the Articles of Organization
The Kentucky Secretary of State’s current fee for domestic LLC Articles of Organization is $40. File through the FastTrack Business Registration Portal or by paper.
4
File the Required Copy With the County Clerk
Kentucky requires an exact or conformed copy of the Articles of Organization to be filed with the county clerk of the county where the LLC’s registered office is located. Current applicable recording fee: $33.
5
Set Up Tax and Compliance Reminders
After formation, determine which Kentucky Department of Revenue accounts apply to the business, including LLET, sales tax, and employer withholding where relevant. Calendar the Secretary of State annual report separately (due June 30).

Choose a Kentucky LLC Name

The legal name must satisfy Kentucky’s LLC naming requirements and be distinguishable from existing business names on state records. Before investing in a domain, signs, or branding, search the Kentucky business records for potential conflicts.

If the LLC is ready to file immediately, reserving the name first is generally unnecessary. If your LLC operates under a name other than its legal name, Kentucky provides a Certificate of Assumed Name for $20.

Appoint a Kentucky Registered Agent

Every Kentucky LLC needs a registered agent and registered office in the Commonwealth. The registered agent receives service of process and official notices for the company.

File the Articles — Then Complete the County Filing

The Kentucky Secretary of State charges $40 for domestic LLC Articles of Organization. You can file online through the FastTrack Business Registration Portal or submit paper Articles.

⚠ Kentucky’s County-Clerk Step Is Easy to Miss: The county-clerk filing is separate from the Secretary of State filing and should be included when calculating the LLC’s practical formation cost. Using the applicable $33 document-tier fee, a standard DIY Kentucky LLC has a baseline formation filing stack of approximately $73 before optional services, licenses, and taxes. Always verify the current county-clerk recording fee before filing.

Kentucky does not require an operating agreement to be filed with the Secretary of State. An internal operating agreement can nevertheless document: ownership percentages, member contributions, voting rights, management authority, distributions, transfers of membership interests, procedures for admitting or removing members, and dissolution procedures.

Kentucky LLC Taxes in 2026

Kentucky’s most distinctive tax issue for many LLC owners is the Limited Liability Entity Tax (LLET). The LLET is separate from the Secretary of State annual report and separate from an owner’s individual income tax.

Tax / Obligation2026 Treatment
Individual income tax rate3.5%
Minimum LLET$175 when applicable
Sales and use tax6% statewide (no local sales tax)
Secretary of State annual report$15

How the $175 Minimum LLET Works

Kentucky calculates LLET using Kentucky gross receipts and Kentucky gross profits, while also applying a small-business threshold based on total gross receipts or total gross profits.

Business LevelGeneral LLET Treatment
$3 million or less in qualifying total gross receipts or gross profitsGenerally pays the $175 minimum
Between $3 million and $6 millionSliding-scale calculation
More than $6 millionFull applicable gross-receipts (0.095%) / gross-profits (0.75%) calculation — the smaller applicable amount
2026 BOI Reporting: A Kentucky LLC created in the United States is currently exempt from FinCEN’s BOI reporting requirement under the August 2026 final rule. U.S. companies are exempt from BOI reporting. Certain foreign entities registered to do business in the United States can still fall within the revised reporting-company definition.

Kentucky LLC Annual Compliance

Kentucky LLCs must file an annual report with the Secretary of State. The filing window runs from January 1 through June 30. The current fee is $15.

🚨 Missed Annual Report = Administrative Dissolution: Kentucky Secretary of State states that domestic entities that fail to file their annual reports by June 30 are administratively dissolved. The company must then go through the reinstatement process and pay delinquent filing fees and applicable penalties. A newly formed Kentucky LLC files its first annual report in the calendar year after the year it was formed.

Closing a Kentucky LLC Properly

Simply stopping business activity does not automatically close a Kentucky LLC. A clean shutdown should address: the Kentucky Secretary of State (Articles of Dissolution — $40), Kentucky Department of Revenue, local licenses and registrations, outstanding taxes, contracts and creditors, and final applicable tax filings. A foreign LLC uses a different filing (Application for Certificate of Withdrawal — $40).

Frequently Asked Questions

1How much does it cost to start an LLC in Kentucky?
The Kentucky Secretary of State filing fee is $40. Kentucky also requires an exact or conformed copy of the Articles of Organization to be filed with the county clerk where the LLC’s registered office is located. Using the applicable $33 county-clerk recording fee gives a $73 baseline filing stack before optional services and other business costs.
2Is the Kentucky LLC annual report annual or biennial?
It is annual. Kentucky annual reports are due by June 30 and the current filing fee is $15.
3Does Kentucky require a county-clerk filing after forming an LLC?
Yes. Kentucky’s Business One Stop instructions state that one file-stamped copy of the Articles of Organization must be filed with the county clerk of the county where the LLC’s registered office is situated.
4Does Kentucky require an operating agreement to be filed with the state?
No. The operating agreement is an internal governance document and is not part of the normal Secretary of State formation filing.
5Can a Kentucky LLC reserve its name before filing?
Yes. Name reservation is optional. Kentucky currently charges $15 for a 120-day name reservation or renewal.
6What is the Kentucky LLET minimum?
For a business subject to the LLET, the minimum is generally $175 per taxable year. Businesses with total gross receipts or total gross profits of $3 million or less generally pay the $175 minimum. Larger businesses use a sliding-scale or full calculation.
7What is Kentucky’s individual income tax rate in 2026?
Kentucky’s individual income tax rate for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2026 is 3.5%.
8Is Kentucky sales tax 6% everywhere in the state?
Kentucky’s sales and use tax rate is 6%, and the Kentucky Department of Revenue states that there are no local sales and use taxes.
9Does a domestic Kentucky LLC have to file a FinCEN BOI report in 2026?
No under the current federal rule. FinCEN’s August 2026 final rule exempts U.S. companies from BOI reporting. Certain foreign entities registered to do business in the United States remain subject to the revised framework.
10How long does it take to form a Kentucky LLC?
The exact processing time depends on the filing method and state processing volume. Kentucky’s formation system supports online filing through FastTrack. Check the Secretary of State’s current instructions for applicable processing expectations before a time-sensitive launch.
11How do I dissolve a Kentucky LLC?
Review the LLC’s governing documents, resolve outstanding obligations, complete applicable final tax filings, and file the required dissolution documents with the Kentucky Secretary of State. The current Secretary of State fee schedule lists $40 for domestic LLC Articles of Dissolution.
