LLC PRICING TRUTH

Can you really start an LLC for $50?

Understanding mandatory government filing fees vs optional third-party service fees.

$50?

Mandatory Government Rates

Sometimes — but the phrase can be misleading. Mandatory state filing fees are set by state governments and cannot be waived.

State Fees vs Advertised Service Fees

A low state filing fee (like $50 in CO or NM) and a low formation-service fee are not the same thing. Mandatory government charges must be paid directly to your Secretary of State.

After Your LLC Is Approved

Getting state approval is step one. To protect your personal liability shield, maintain IRS tax compliance, and open your bank account, complete these 6 essential steps in order:

STEP 01 FREE IRS STEP

Get Federal EIN (SS-4 Tax ID)

Your 9-digit IRS tax ID is required to open business bank accounts, hire workers, and issue 1099/W2 forms. Free directly from the IRS website in 5 minutes.

Apply Free at IRS.gov →
STEP 02 LEGAL SHIELD

Draft Operating Agreement

Internal legal document that proves your LLC is a separate corporate entity. Outlines ownership percentages, member voting rights, and profit splits.

Download Free Template →
STEP 03 CRITICAL

Open Business Checking Account

Never mix personal and business money. Commingling funds pierces your corporate liability veil, making your personal assets vulnerable in lawsuits.

STEP 04 FEDERAL MANDATE

File FinCEN BOI Report

Federal law requires filing a Beneficial Ownership Information report with FinCEN within 90 days of formation. Avoid severe $500/day federal late penalties.

DECISION GUIDANCE

Straight answers before you form your LLC

These are the decisions that repeatedly confuse first-time owners. Click any question below to expand the direct answer.

01 Should I form in Wyoming or Delaware if I live somewhere else?

Usually not. If you actually operate in your home state, forming elsewhere can mean foreign qualification, paying two annual fees, maintaining two registered agents, and filing extra compliance forms.

See which state makes sense for you →
02 Do I need an LLC before I make my first dollar?

Not every business must begin as an LLC. Sole proprietorships are free to start, but offer zero personal asset liability protection. Once you begin taking customer payments or facing legal risks, forming an LLC creates an official corporate liability shield.

LLC vs sole proprietorship breakdown →
03 Can I form an LLC myself instead of paying a service?

Yes! You can file Articles of Organization directly with your Secretary of State online. A formation agency is optional and mainly useful if you want assistance with registered agent address privacy, document drafting, or filing.

Find your 50-state filing guide →
04 Do I get the Federal EIN before or after forming the LLC?

After. The IRS requires your official state legal entity (Articles of Organization) to be approved before applying for your free Federal Employer Identification Number (EIN / SS-4).

Follow the step-by-step EIN guide →
05 Do I really need a registered agent?

Yes. All 50 states mandate that LLCs maintain a registered agent with a physical street address in the formation state. You can act as your own registered agent for $0, or hire a commercial service (~$125/yr) to keep your personal home address off public state records.

Read registered agent rules & options →
06 Can I really get an LLC for only $50?

In states like Colorado or New Mexico, the mandatory state filing fee is $50. However, advertised "$0 + state fee" or "$50 LLC" packages from third-party services often exclude mandatory Secretary of State fees ($35 - $500). Always check total state costs before filing.
