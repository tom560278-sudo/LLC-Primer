Editorial Policy & Standards

Our Commitment to Accuracy: LLC Primer is an independent educational publisher dedicated to helping founders understand LLC requirements in all 50 U.S. states. Every fee, statutory deadline, and filing rule published on our site is grounded in primary government sources.

1. Primary Government Sources First

State business entity laws, filing fees, and annual reporting requirements change frequently. Our research methodology prioritizes primary, authoritative sources:

  • Secretary of State & Business Division Portals: We verify formation filing fees, annual report fees, expedited fees, and online portals directly with each state's corporate division.
  • State Revenue & Tax Agencies: State franchise taxes, entity excise taxes, and gross receipts taxes are verified with official state tax department schedules (such as the California Franchise Tax Board, Delaware Division of Revenue, and Texas Comptroller).
  • Internal Revenue Service (IRS): All EIN, federal entity classification, and partnership/corporate tax election guidance is verified against official IRS publications and forms.

2. Independence from Commercial Partnerships

While LLC Primer participates in affiliate programs with reputable business formation and registered agent providers, our commercial relationships never determine our editorial ratings, state fee calculations, or educational analysis.

We do not accept payment to alter filing facts, misrepresent state fees, or hide statutory requirements. If a state allows founders to file directly online for a low fee (or $0 annual reports, such as in Mississippi or Montana during the waiver window), we clearly state that fact and link directly to the official government portal.

3. Regular Review and Fact-Checking Process

State legislative changes (such as Pennsylvania's Act 122 annual report transition or the expiration of California's AB 85 first-year franchise tax exemption) require ongoing monitoring. Our editorial team conducts periodic audits of all 50 state guides, fee calculators, and compliance tools.

We display review dates on our state guides and calculators to reflect when statutory information was last verified against primary sources. If you have questions regarding our editorial standards, primary research sources, or fact-checking methodology, please contact our editorial desk at editorial@llcprimer.com.

4. Corrections and Reporting Errors

We believe transparency is fundamental to publisher credibility. If you notice any factual errors, outdated information, or incorrect filing fees across our state guides, please report them to our editorial desk at editorial@llcprimer.com or reach out through our Contact Page. We review all submitted corrections promptly against official state records and publish necessary updates.

5. Educational Disclaimer

LLC Primer publishes general educational guides, cost calculators, and compliance checklists. We are not an attorney, CPA, or government agency. Our content does not constitute formal legal, accounting, or tax advice. For specific organizational structuring or complex tax planning, we encourage founders to consult with a licensed professional in their jurisdiction.

Learn more about our review process on our How We Review page or review our full Affiliate Disclosure.
