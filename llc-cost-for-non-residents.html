US LLC Cost for Non-US Residents: Quick Answer

Last reviewed: September 7, 2026  ·  Official source: IRS Form 5472 guidance provides the primary guidance used in this guide.

A US LLC for a non-US resident typically costs between $141 and $500 to start, depending on the state and whether you form the company yourself or use a formation service.

For most foreign entrepreneurs, the lowest-cost option is a DIY Wyoming LLC. In that scenario, you pay the state filing fee, maintain a registered agent, and apply for an EIN directly with the IRS. The total startup cost can be as low as $141.

If you prefer a done-for-you solution that includes EIN assistance, compliance guidance, registered agent service, and banking support, expect to spend $300–500 during the first year. Ongoing annual costs are usually between $100 and $600, depending on the state, registered agent provider, and tax compliance requirements.

Quick summary: Most non-US founders spend $300–1,200 during their first year when formation costs, registered agent fees, banking setup, and Form 5472 compliance are included. Wyoming remains the most popular choice because it combines low filing fees, strong privacy protections, and relatively low annual maintenance costs.

The LLC cost for non-US residents breaks into two buckets: what you pay once to form the LLC, and what you pay every year to keep it alive. The minimum realistic first-year cost in Wyoming — the most popular state for non-residents — is around $141 if you handle everything yourself. With a formation service that covers EIN, registered agent, and compliance needs, expect $300–500. Annual costs run $99–250 depending on your registered agent and compliance needs.

How much does a US LLC cost for non-US residents?

It depends on the state, but here is the honest range across all scenarios:

  • Minimum (Wyoming, DIY): ~$141 Year 1 — $100 filing + $2 online fee + $39 registered agent. EIN is free from the IRS.
  • Typical (Wyoming, formation service): $300–500 Year 1 — includes state fee, registered agent, EIN filing, operating agreement, and banking setup guidance.
  • Annual maintenance (Wyoming): $101–160 — $62 annual report + $39–100 registered agent renewal.
  • Full compliance (all-in): $600–1,200 Year 1 — adds Form 5472 CPA preparation, a virtual office address, and accounting setup.

Non-US residents pay the same state filing fees as US residents. What adds cost is the EIN process (if you want help), the mandatory registered agent (non-residents can't be their own), and federal compliance requirements specific to foreign-owned LLCs — most notably Form 5472.

Ongoing annual costs for a foreign-owned LLC

These costs repeat every year your LLC stays in existence. Missing them triggers loss of good standing, administrative dissolution, or IRS penalties.

Annual report / license tax

Almost every state except New Mexico charges an annual renewal fee. In Wyoming, the minimum is $62/year (with online convenience fee), due the first day of the anniversary month of formation. In Delaware, it's a flat $300 franchise tax due June 1, with a $200 late penalty. Texas charges $0 in annual report fees for most LLCs but imposes a franchise tax on entities with revenue above $2.47 million.

Registered agent renewal

Your registered agent must be maintained year-round. Northwest charges $39/year — the lowest flat rate among reputable services. Budget $39–150/year depending on provider.

Form 5472 — mandatory for foreign-owned single-member LLCs

This is the most commonly missed annual expense. Every single-member LLC owned by a non-US person must file Form 5472 plus a pro forma Form 1120 by April 15 each year, regardless of revenue. The IRS does not send reminders. A CPA experienced with foreign-owned entities charges $200–500 for this filing. See the LLC taxes guide for the full compliance picture.

Is an EIN free?

The IRS does not charge for EIN issuance. Applying directly through the IRS costs nothing. The catch for non-residents: the online EIN application requires an SSN or ITIN. Non-residents apply via fax (Form SS-4) or by calling the IRS international line — both are free but take 3–6 weeks in 2026.

Third-party EIN services charge $50–200 to prepare and submit your Form SS-4 for you. They have no faster channel to the IRS — they submit the same fax, wait the same 3–6 weeks. You pay for coordination, not speed.

No service can accelerate IRS EIN processing for non-residents. If a service promises a fast EIN without an SSN, it either uses a workaround you shouldn't touch or is making a claim it cannot deliver. The 3–6 week IRS timeline is fixed.

A business address is separate from your registered agent address. Mercury Bank no longer accepts an RA address as your business address — you need a distinct US business address for banking applications.

Hidden costs most founders miss

  • Form 5472 filing ($200–500/year) — every single-member foreign-owned LLC must file this annually. Missing it costs $25,000 per violation. Most guides don't mention this until after formation.
  • Bank account setup time — Mercury, Relay, and Wise do not charge for basic accounts, but some founders pay $15–50/month for business checking with premium features. Budget for this.
  • Operating agreement legal review ($100–400) — a template operating agreement is fine for basic LLCs, but if you have multiple members or complex ownership, professional review is worth the cost.
  • State foreign qualification fees — if your LLC operates in a state other than where it was formed, you may need to register as a foreign LLC there. Fees range from $50–500 depending on the state.
  • Annual report late fees — Wyoming charges $2/month for late annual reports. Delaware charges $200 for late franchise tax payments. Florida has a $400 late fee. Set calendar reminders.
  • Stripe and payment processor verification costs — not monetary, but time-consuming. Budget 1–2 weeks for Stripe verification after your EIN is confirmed.

Common cost mistakes

  • Forgetting Form 5472 each year. This is the most expensive mistake. The $25,000 IRS penalty for a missed Form 5472 is real and automatic. Budget $200–500/year for a CPA to prepare it.
  • Choosing Delaware without a business reason. Delaware's $300/year franchise tax makes it significantly more expensive than Wyoming for businesses that don't need Delaware's corporate law advantages.
  • Paying for an EIN service when the IRS is free. Third-party EIN services ($50–200) submit the same fax to the IRS that you can send yourself. They do not speed up the process.
  • Missing annual report deadlines. Late fees range from $2/month (Wyoming) to $400 (Florida). Set a calendar reminder for your LLC's anniversary month.
  • Not budgeting for a business address. Mercury Bank requires a real US business address, not an RA address. Factor in $0 (Northwest RA includes one) to $50/month depending on provider.
  • Underestimating Year 2+ costs. First-year costs often look low because some services waive fees or bundle them. Budget $100–600/year in recurring costs from Year 2 onwards.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a Wyoming LLC cost for a non-US resident in 2026?
The minimum Year 1 cost is approximately $141: $102 state filing fee + $39 for Northwest Registered Agent. EIN is free from the IRS. Annual maintenance from Year 2 is $101/yr minimum ($62 annual report + $39 RA). Add $200–500/yr for Form 5472 CPA preparation.
Do non-US residents pay more to form an LLC than US residents?
State filing fees are the same for everyone. What adds cost for non-residents: mandatory commercial registered agent (can't be your own), EIN application assistance (optional but often helpful), and Form 5472 annual filing (mandatory for foreign-owned single-member LLCs).
What is the cheapest US LLC for a non-US resident?
New Mexico has the lowest Year 1 cost at approximately $89 ($50 filing + $39 RA) with no annual report requirement. Wyoming is $141 Year 1 with a $62/yr annual report. Wyoming is generally preferred because it has better banking reputation and privacy laws.
Is Form 5472 really mandatory for foreign-owned LLCs?
Yes. Every single-member LLC with a non-US owner must file Form 5472 (with a pro forma Form 1120) by April 15 each year, regardless of whether the LLC earned any money. The IRS does not send reminders. Missing it triggers a $25,000 automatic penalty per violation.
Can I be my own registered agent as a non-US resident?
No. A registered agent must have a physical address in the formation state and must be available during business hours to accept legal documents. As a non-resident, you do not have a state address, so you must use a commercial registered agent service.
Does doola include Form 5472 filing?
doola includes Form 5472 preparation in their compliance plans (not all formation plans). Check their current plan details before purchasing — compliance plans typically cost more than basic formation packages but cover the annual IRS filing requirement.
