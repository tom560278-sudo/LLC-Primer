How to Start an LLC in Maine in 2026

Last reviewed: September 30, 2026  ·  Primary source: Maine Secretary of State, Division of Corporations, UCC and Commissions

Maine charges $175 to form a domestic LLC using Form MLLC-6 (Certificate of Formation). Maine’s Secretary of State currently lists standard corporate filing processing at approximately 15–20 business days, while expedited processing is available for an additional fee. Maine also has an important requirement that differs from many states: state law requires an LLC to have a limited liability company agreement (operating agreement). The agreement may be entered into before, after, or at the time the Certificate of Formation is filed.

Maine LLCs must file an annual report every year by June 1. The current fee for a domestic LLC annual report is $85. The first annual report is due between January 1 and June 1 of the year following the calendar year in which the LLC was formed.

Step 2: Appoint a Registered Agent

Maine LLCs must maintain a registered agent with an appropriate Maine physical address. The state’s Certificate of Formation identifies the registered agent as either a commercial or noncommercial registered agent and requires a physical location rather than a P.O. Box.

Step 3: File the Certificate of Formation

The Certificate of Formation (Form MLLC-6) creates a domestic Maine LLC. Current filing fee: $175. Submit to the Secretary of State, Division of Corporations, UCC and Commissions, 101 State House Station, Augusta, ME 04333-0101. The form must be signed by at least one authorized person.

Step 4: Create Your LLC Agreement

Maine is unusual because state law specifically requires a limited liability company agreement. Under 31 MRSA §1531, an LLC must have an LLC agreement. The agreement can be entered into or otherwise exist before, after, or at the time of filing the Certificate of Formation. It does not have to be filed with the Secretary of State.

Step 5: Get Your EIN

An EIN is the federal tax identification number issued by the IRS. The IRS issues EINs directly without a federal application fee. An LLC may need an EIN for federal tax purposes, hiring employees, banking, or other business activities depending on its structure and circumstances. See our EIN guide.

Step 6: Open a Business Bank Account

A separate business bank account can help keep LLC transactions organized. Banks commonly request: approved Certificate of Formation (or equivalent evidence), EIN confirmation, LLC Agreement, and government-issued identification. The federal and Maine tax treatment of an LLC depends on its classification and elections. For Maine individual income tax, the 2026 rate schedule has three main brackets. Maine Revenue Services also lists a 2% surcharge on the portion of Maine taxable income above $1 million for tax years beginning in 2026.

Annual Report and Compliance Requirements

Maine requires domestic LLCs to file an annual report every year to maintain good standing. The legal deadline is June 1, and the current fee for a domestic LLC is $85. The first annual report is due between January 1 and June 1 of the year following the calendar year in which the LLC was formed. For example, if you form a Maine LLC at any point during 2026, the first annual report is due by June 1, 2027.

🚨 Do Not Wait Until the Final Day (Paper Filers): Maine specifically advises paper filers near the June 1 deadline to use mailing methods that document when the filing was delivered to the USPS. Continued failure to satisfy state filing requirements can result in loss of good standing.

Common Mistakes When Starting a Maine LLC

Mistake 1 — Using an Outdated Formation Form: Always download the current version of MLLC-6 from the Secretary of State before filing. The state specifically warns that forms can be revised or eliminated.
Mistake 2 — Forgetting the LLC Agreement: Maine law requires an LLC agreement. Unlike many other states where an operating agreement is primarily recommended, Maine’s statute expressly requires an agreement to exist.
Mistake 3 — Using an Invalid Registered-Agent Address: The Maine Certificate of Formation specifies that the physical location of the registered agent cannot be a P.O. Box.
Mistake 4 — Assuming the Annual Report Is Optional: Maine requires an annual report every year to maintain good standing. The deadline is June 1, and the domestic LLC fee is currently $85.

Frequently Asked Questions

1How much does it cost to start an LLC in Maine?
The current state filing fee for a domestic Maine LLC is $175. An optional name reservation costs $20, and the annual report for a domestic LLC costs $85. Registered-agent services and other business services are separate costs.
2How long does it take to form an LLC in Maine?
Maine’s Secretary of State currently lists corporate filing processing at approximately 15–20 business days. Expedited service is available: $50 additional for 24-hour service or $100 additional for immediate same-business-day service.
3Can I expedite my Maine LLC filing?
Yes. Maine’s Secretary of State provides expedited filing options. The current additional fee is $50 for 24-hour service or $100 for immediate same-business-day service, subject to the state’s processing rules and workload.
4Does Maine require an Operating Agreement for LLCs?
Maine law requires a limited liability company agreement. Under 31 MRSA §1531, the agreement may be entered into before, after, or at the time the Certificate of Formation is filed. It does not need to be filed with the Secretary of State.
5Do I need a registered agent for my Maine LLC?
Yes. The Maine Certificate of Formation requires registered-agent information and provides for a commercial or noncommercial registered agent. The registered office must have a physical location in Maine rather than a P.O. Box.
6When is the Maine LLC annual report due?
The annual report is due June 1 every year. The current fee for a domestic LLC is $85. The first report is due between January 1 and June 1 of the year following the calendar year in which the LLC was formed.
7Can I file the Maine annual report online?
Yes. Maine provides an online annual-report filing system for LLCs and other covered business entities. Paper filing is also available. Note: do not wait until the final day if filing by mail — Maine specifically advises paper filers near the June 1 deadline to use mailing methods that document when the filing was delivered to the USPS.
8How is a Maine LLC taxed?
The tax treatment depends on the LLC’s federal tax classification and Maine tax obligations. For individual Maine income tax in 2026, the published rates are 5.8%, 6.75%, and 7.15%, with thresholds adjusted for inflation. Maine also applies a 2% surcharge on applicable taxable income above the statutory high-income thresholds beginning in 2026.
9What is Maine’s sales tax rate?
Maine’s general sales tax rate is 5.5% in 2026. Certain categories, such as prepared food and lodging, have different rates.
10Can I form a Maine LLC if I don’t live in Maine?
Maine’s formation process does not require the LLC owner to personally live in Maine. However, the LLC must satisfy the state’s registered-agent requirements, including maintaining an appropriate Maine physical location for the registered agent. If the LLC actually conducts business in another state, also check whether that state requires the Maine LLC to register as a foreign LLC.
