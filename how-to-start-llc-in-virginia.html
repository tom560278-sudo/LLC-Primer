How to Start an LLC in Virginia in 2026

Last reviewed: September 2026  ·  Primary sources: Virginia State Corporation Commission, Virginia Tax

Virginia LLCs are formed through the Virginia State Corporation Commission (SCC) rather than a Secretary of State. The SCC’s Clerk’s Information System (CIS) is the state’s online system for forming an LLC, maintaining business records, and making many subsequent filings and payments.

The standard Virginia LLC formation fee is $100. Virginia LLCs also pay a $50 annual registration fee each year after formation. The fee is due by the last day of the month in which the LLC was originally organized. Virginia does not require LLCs to file an annual report with the SCC.

Virginia also has an important distinction between state and local compliance. There is no single statewide general business license that applies to every business, but cities and counties may impose local business-license or BPOL (Business, Professional and Occupational License) requirements. Local rules, thresholds, and tax rates vary, so your LLC’s location and business activity matter.

Virginia Uses the SCC, Not a Secretary of State: Virginia’s business-formation agency is the State Corporation Commission (SCC). The SCC’s Clerk’s Information System (CIS) provides the state’s online business filing system. Use the SCC’s official resources when checking formation fees, business-name availability, registered-agent information, and annual registration requirements. The SCC currently lists $100 as the fee to form a Virginia LLC.

Step 1: Choose a Name for Your Virginia LLC

Your first step is choosing a business name that complies with Virginia’s LLC naming requirements. The name should be distinguishable from other business names already on the SCC’s records and must contain an appropriate LLC designator, such as “Limited Liability Company,” “LLC,” or “L.L.C.” Before filing, search the SCC’s business records through CIS to check whether your preferred name is available. The SCC currently lists the name-reservation filing as SCC631 and the fee as $10 for a 120-day reservation. A reservation does not create an LLC — it simply provides additional time to prepare for formation.

Fictitious or Assumed Business Name: If your LLC will operate under a business name different from its legal name, you may need to register a fictitious name with the SCC. Check the current SCC requirements before filing because fictitious-name rules are separate from forming the LLC itself.

Step 2: Appoint a Registered Agent

Virginia LLCs must maintain a registered agent and registered office in Virginia. The registered agent receives official notices and service of process for the LLC. The registered office must be a physical Virginia address that satisfies the state’s requirements — a P.O. Box alone is not sufficient.

Step 3: File the Articles of Organization

Virginia LLCs are formed by filing Articles of Organization (Form LLC1011) with the Virginia SCC. The SCC lists the standard formation fee as $100. You can file online through CIS or submit the appropriate paper form.

Step 4: Create an Operating Agreement

Virginia does not require every LLC to file an operating agreement with the SCC. Even when it is not filed with the state, an operating agreement can document how the owners intend to operate the business. A multi-member LLC should generally consider documenting its ownership and management arrangements before beginning significant business activity.

Step 5: Apply for an EIN

An Employer Identification Number (EIN) is issued by the IRS. Depending on the LLC’s tax classification and circumstances, an EIN may be needed for activities such as hiring employees, opening a business bank account, or meeting federal tax-filing requirements. The IRS issues EINs at no charge when obtained directly from the IRS. If you are a non-U.S. applicant who is not eligible to use the IRS online EIN application, different application procedures may apply. Do not pay a third party simply to obtain an EIN when you can apply directly through the IRS.

Step 6: Open a Business Bank Account

After formation, open a dedicated business bank account and keep business transactions separate from personal finances. Banks and financial institutions may request: LLC formation documents, EIN confirmation, operating agreement, government-issued identification, and additional ownership or beneficial-owner information. Requirements vary by financial institution, so check with the bank before opening the account.

Virginia LLC Taxes Explained

Forming an LLC does not by itself determine how the business will be taxed. Understanding how LLCs are taxed at the federal level is the starting point before evaluating Virginia-specific obligations.

BPOL and Local Business Taxes: Virginia allows localities to impose BPOL taxes or license requirements, but the rules are not uniform across the Commonwealth. Your city or county may establish its own BPOL requirements, gross-receipts thresholds, tax rates, filing deadlines, and business-license procedures. Virginia Tax specifically notes that localities have discretion regarding BPOL requirements, rates, and thresholds within the statutory framework. Check your city or county before assuming that your LLC has no local business-tax or licensing obligations.
Virginia Sales Tax: Virginia’s general combined sales-tax rate is 5.3%, consisting of the 4.3% state rate and 1% local rate. Certain regions have additional sales taxes — Northern Virginia and Hampton Roads generally have a combined rate of 6%. If your LLC sells taxable goods or services, check Virginia Tax’s current registration and collection requirements before making taxable sales.

Minimum State Formation Cost: $100
For a basic Virginia LLC formed without optional services, the initial state formation cost is $100. After formation, the SCC annual registration fee is $50 per year beginning the year after formation. There is no annual report fee.

Annual Registration Fee and Ongoing Compliance

Virginia LLCs do not file an annual report with the SCC. Instead, each Virginia LLC must pay a $50 annual registration fee after the year in which it was formed. The fee is due by the last day of the month in which the LLC was organized. The SCC assesses the fee approximately two months before the due date and sends the assessment to the registered agent at the registered office on file.

What Happens If You Miss the Annual Registration Fee? The SCC imposes a $25 penalty when the annual registration fee is not received by the due date. If the fee remains unpaid through the last day of the third month following the due date, the SCC states that a Virginia LLC’s existence will be automatically canceled by operation of law. Keep your registered-agent information current and monitor your LLC’s status through CIS rather than relying exclusively on mailed notices.

Common Mistakes When Starting a Virginia LLC

Mistake 1: Looking for a Secretary of State Filing
Virginia LLCs are handled by the State Corporation Commission, not a Secretary of State. Use the SCC’s official business resources and CIS system when checking Virginia LLC requirements.
Mistake 2: Assuming There Is an Annual Report
Virginia LLCs pay a $50 annual registration fee, but they do not file an annual report with the SCC.
Mistake 3: Forgetting Local Requirements
State formation does not automatically satisfy every local business-license or tax requirement. Check the city or county where your business operates, particularly for BPOL requirements.
Mistake 4: Missing the Annual Registration Fee
The SCC imposes a $25 late penalty, and continued nonpayment can result in cancellation of the LLC.
Mistake 5: Treating an S-Corp Election as Automatic Tax Savings
An S-corp election changes the way an LLC is taxed; it does not automatically reduce taxes for every owner. Consider the federal and Virginia consequences with a qualified tax professional before making the election.
Virginia LLC — Bottom Line

A Virginia LLC starts with a $100 SCC formation filing and requires a $50 annual registration fee after the year of formation. LLCs do not file an annual report with the SCC. The most important practical distinction is that Virginia’s state-level LLC requirements are handled through the SCC, while many business-license and tax obligations can exist separately at the local level. Before filing, check the current SCC requirements, confirm your business name, appoint a qualifying registered agent, and identify any local licensing or tax requirements that apply to your business.

Frequently Asked Questions

1How much does it cost to start an LLC in Virginia?
The SCC formation fee is $100. After the year of formation, the annual registration fee is $50 per year. An optional name reservation costs $10. An EIN is free through the IRS. Other costs can include registered-agent service, local BPOL or licensing fees, professional services, and insurance.
2Where do I file a Virginia LLC?
You file a Virginia LLC with the Virginia State Corporation Commission (SCC), not a Secretary of State. The SCC’s Clerk’s Information System (CIS) is the online filing system.
3Does Virginia require an annual report for LLCs?
No. Virginia LLCs do not file an annual report with the SCC. Instead, each LLC pays a $50 annual registration fee due by the last day of the month in which the LLC was originally organized.
4What happens if I miss the Virginia annual registration fee?
The SCC imposes a $25 late penalty. If the fee remains unpaid through the last day of the third month following the due date, the SCC states that the Virginia LLC’s existence will be automatically canceled by operation of law.
5What are Virginia’s individual income tax rates?
Virginia’s individual income tax rates currently range from 2% to 5.75%. The top rate of 5.75% applies to taxable income above $17,000 under Virginia’s published individual income-tax schedule.
6Does Virginia have a BPOL tax?
Virginia allows localities to impose Business, Professional and Occupational License (BPOL) taxes or license requirements. Local rules, thresholds, rates, and filing deadlines are not uniform across the Commonwealth. Check with your specific city or county before assuming your LLC has no local business-tax obligations.
7What is Virginia’s sales tax rate?
Virginia’s general combined sales-tax rate is 5.3% (4.3% state + 1% local). Northern Virginia and Hampton Roads generally have a combined rate of 6%. Businesses with taxable sales must register with Virginia Tax and collect the applicable rate.
8Can I use expedited processing for a Virginia LLC?
Yes. The SCC offers online next-day expedited service (additional fee of $50 or $100 depending on filing type) and same-day service ($200 additional; submit by 10:00 AM SCC cutoff). Expedited fees are nonrefundable. Expedited services are only available for filings submitted through CIS.
