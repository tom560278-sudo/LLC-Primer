How to Start an LLC in Mississippi in 2026

Last reviewed: October 2026  ·  Primary sources: Mississippi Secretary of State, Mississippi Department of Revenue

Mississippi LLC formation starts with a $50 state filing fee. Domestic LLC annual reports are required each year but are free to file and generally due by April 15. Mississippi business filings are handled through the Secretary of State's online filing system.

For 2026, Mississippi's individual income-tax structure applies a 0% rate to the first $10,000 of taxable income and a 4% rate to taxable income above $10,000. The tax treatment of LLC income depends on the LLC's federal tax classification and the owner's circumstances.

A Mississippi LLC can therefore be formed for the $50 state filing fee if you handle the formation yourself, serve as your own registered agent when eligible, and do not purchase optional services.

Mississippi LLC Income Tax in 2026

Mississippi's individual income-tax structure changed as part of the state's tax-reduction legislation. For tax year 2026: First $10,000 of taxable income 0% | Taxable income above $10,000 4%.

The rate applies to taxable income rather than simply the amount of money an LLC receives or distributes. Example: if a taxpayer has $60,000 of Mississippi taxable income, the first $10,000 is taxed at 0% and the remaining $50,000 is subject to the 4% rate, producing $2,000 of Mississippi individual income tax before considering other applicable rules or credits. Do not assume that an LLC's gross revenue or distributions automatically equal taxable income. Understand how LLCs are taxed before making tax assumptions.

1. Choose a Name for Your Mississippi LLC

Mississippi LLC naming requirements are established by Mississippi law. Your LLC name must satisfy the state's requirements and be distinguishable from other entities on the Secretary of State's records. The name generally must contain "Limited Liability Company," "LLC," or "L.L.C." Certain words and phrases may require additional authorization or may be restricted.

Mississippi LLC Naming Requirements

Check Name Availability: Use the Mississippi Secretary of State Business Search (link: https://corp.sos.ms.gov/corp/portal/c/page/corpBusinessIdSearch/portal.aspx) to search existing business records. Search several variations of your proposed name before filing.

Name Reservation — $25: Mississippi allows an applicant to reserve an available business name for 180 days. The current fee is $25. If you are ready to form the LLC, you can generally proceed directly to the Certificate of Formation.

2. Appoint a Registered Agent

Every Mississippi LLC must maintain a registered agent and registered office in Mississippi. The registered agent receives official notices, service of process, and other legal or state correspondence for the LLC.

Professional Registered Agent Services: A commercial registered-agent service can be useful if you do not have a suitable Mississippi address, do not want your personal address used for the registered office, or want a service to receive state correspondence.

4. Create an Operating Agreement

Mississippi does not generally require an LLC to file an operating agreement with the Secretary of State. An operating agreement is still an important internal document.

What to Include:

  • Ownership percentages
  • Initial capital contributions
  • Profit and loss allocations
  • Distributions
  • Voting rights
  • Management responsibilities
  • Authority to enter contracts
  • Adding new members
  • Member withdrawal
  • Transfer of ownership interests
  • Buyout procedures
  • Dissolution procedures
  • Dispute-resolution procedures

For a multi-member LLC, putting the agreed ownership and management terms in writing can reduce uncertainty later.

5. Apply for an EIN

An Employer Identification Number (EIN) is issued by the IRS. Many LLCs need an EIN for federal tax purposes, hiring employees, banking, or other business activities.

The IRS does not charge a fee for an EIN. Apply directly through the IRS EIN application: https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number

Avoid Paid EIN Services When You Can Apply Directly: The IRS issues EINs for free. Third-party companies may charge a fee for submitting an application on your behalf.

6. Open a Business Bank Account

Keeping business and personal finances separate is an important part of maintaining clear business records. Establishing a dedicated business bank account for your LLC is highly recommended.

Benefits:

  • Track business income and expenses
  • Maintain cleaner accounting records
  • Demonstrate separation between the LLC and its owners
  • Pay business expenses from business funds
  • Make tax preparation easier

Documents a Bank May Request: Approved Certificate of Formation | EIN confirmation | Operating agreement | Government-issued identification | Ownership information | Business address information

7. Mississippi LLC Taxes — Full Breakdown

Mississippi LLC taxation depends on the LLC's federal tax classification and the type of activity it conducts. Understand how LLCs are taxed for more context on the basic rules.

EINFree

Pass-Through Taxation: A single-member LLC is generally treated as a disregarded entity for federal income-tax purposes unless it makes an election to be taxed differently. A multi-member LLC is generally treated as a partnership for federal tax purposes unless it elects another classification.

Mississippi Corporate Franchise Tax: Mississippi imposes a franchise tax under its corporate tax rules. For 2026, the corporate franchise-tax rate is $0.50 per $1,000 of taxable capital, subject to the applicable statutory minimum and calculation rules. An ordinary pass-through LLC should not automatically be described as owing corporate franchise tax simply because it is an LLC. If an LLC has elected corporate tax treatment or falls under another applicable classification, consult the Mississippi Department of Revenue or a qualified tax professional.

Mississippi Sales Tax: Mississippi's general state sales-tax rate is 7%. Whether your LLC must register, collect, and remit sales tax depends on what the business sells and the applicable Mississippi tax rules. Use the Mississippi Department of Revenue for current registration, filing, and payment requirements: https://www.dor.ms.gov/

9. File Your Annual Report — Free and Due April 15

Mississippi LLCs must file an annual report with the Secretary of State. For domestic LLCs, the annual report filing fee is $0. The annual report deadline is generally April 15.

What to Do Each Year: Log into the Mississippi Secretary of State business filing system | Locate your LLC | Review the information shown | Update information that has changed | Submit the annual report | Save confirmation.

What Happens If You Miss the Annual Report? Failure to file required annual reports can cause the LLC to lose good standing and may ultimately lead to administrative dissolution under Mississippi law. A free annual report is therefore still an important compliance requirement. Do not treat the $0 filing fee as meaning the filing is optional.

11. Common Mistakes When Starting an LLC in Mississippi

1. Assuming a Name Search Guarantees Approval: Search is useful but doesn't guarantee approval.

2. Using an Ineligible Registered Agent: Must have qualifying registered agent and physical registered-office address. No P.O. Box alone.

3. Forgetting the Annual Report: Free but mandatory. Mark April 15 on compliance calendar every year.

4. Assuming All LLC Income Is Taxed the Same Way: Depends on federal tax classification and owner's circumstances.

5. Mixing Personal and Business Finances: Use dedicated business account.

6. Assuming an LLC Automatically Has Every Required License: Check Mississippi state requirements and city/county requirements.

12. Frequently Asked Questions

1How much does it cost to start an LLC in Mississippi?
$50 Certificate of Formation. Annual report free. Optional: registered-agent service, name reservation, licenses, permits, insurance, professional services, banking.
2How long does it take to form an LLC in Mississippi?
Depends on Secretary of State workload and filing method. Verify current processing information through Mississippi Secretary of State.
3Does Mississippi require an annual report for LLCs?
Yes, generally by April 15 each year. Domestic LLC annual reports are free.
4What is Mississippi's LLC filing fee in 2026?
$50. Verify final amount displayed by official filing system.
5Does Mississippi require a registered agent?
Yes. A Mississippi LLC must maintain a registered agent and registered office satisfying state requirements.
6Can I be my own registered agent in Mississippi?
Yes, if you meet the state's requirements and maintain the required Mississippi address and availability.
7Does Mississippi require an operating agreement?
Not required to be filed. Creating one is still recommended.
8Does Mississippi have a franchise tax for LLCs?
Mississippi has a corporate franchise tax. Whether an LLC is subject depends on its tax classification. A standard pass-through LLC should not automatically be treated as owing the corporate franchise tax.
9What is Mississippi's individual income-tax rate in 2026?
0% on first $10,000; 4% above $10,000. Individual income-tax structure; actual treatment depends on LLC classification.
10What is Mississippi's sales-tax rate?
7% general state rate. Whether a particular LLC must collect depends on products, services, and applicable tax rules.
11Can a non-U.S. resident form an LLC in Mississippi?
A non-U.S. resident can generally participate. Formation, registered-agent, federal tax, banking, and immigration considerations are separate issues. For international founders, read our guide on forming a U.S. LLC as a non-resident.
12Where do I file a Mississippi LLC?
Through the Mississippi Secretary of State's business filing system: https://www.sos.ms.gov/business-services-regulation
