How to Start an LLC in Massachusetts in 2026

Last reviewed: September 2026  ·  Primary sources: Massachusetts Secretary of the Commonwealth, Massachusetts Department of Revenue

Massachusetts charges $500 to file a Certificate of Organization for a domestic LLC. Each Massachusetts LLC must also file an annual report for $500 every year, with the report generally due on the anniversary of the LLC’s formation. Knowing how to start an LLC in Massachusetts means understanding the filing requirements, recurring costs, resident-agent rules, tax obligations, and ongoing compliance deadlines before you form the business.

The formation process is relatively straightforward. You choose a distinguishable business name, appoint a Massachusetts resident agent, file a Certificate of Organization with the Secretary of the Commonwealth, create an operating agreement, obtain an EIN when required, and separate the LLC’s finances from your personal finances.

Build recurring compliance costs into your business budget before forming the LLC.

Step 1: Choose a Name for Your Massachusetts LLC

Your Massachusetts LLC name must comply with Massachusetts naming requirements and be distinguishable from names already registered with the Commonwealth. Before filing, search the Massachusetts business database to identify potentially conflicting names.

Massachusetts DBA Note: If your LLC conducts business under a name different from its legal name, Massachusetts generally requires a business certificate (DBA) through the appropriate city or town. Requirements and fees are handled locally, not through the Secretary of the Commonwealth.

Step 2: Appoint a Resident Agent

Massachusetts uses the term resident agent for the person or organization designated to receive legal and official documents for an LLC. A Massachusetts LLC must continuously maintain an eligible resident agent and a Massachusetts address for service of process.

An owner can potentially serve as the LLC’s resident agent if the legal requirements are satisfied. A professional resident-agent company provides a dedicated business address and receives legal documents on behalf of the LLC. Step 3: File the Certificate of Organization

The Certificate of Organization is the document used to create a Massachusetts LLC. Filed with the Massachusetts Secretary of the Commonwealth Corporations Division. Standard filing fee: $500.

Professional LLCs Note: Certain licensed professional activities can be subject to additional Massachusetts requirements. If your business provides a regulated professional service (legal, medical, architectural, engineering, etc.), verify the applicable professional-entity rules with the relevant Massachusetts licensing authority before filing.

Step 4: Create an Operating Agreement

Massachusetts does not require you to file an operating agreement with the Secretary of the Commonwealth. An operating agreement is nevertheless an important internal document for an LLC. It can establish ownership, management authority, voting procedures, profit distributions, member contributions, transfers, and procedures for handling changes in ownership.

Step 5: Get Your EIN

An EIN is a federal tax identification number issued by the IRS. The IRS provides EIN applications directly and does not charge a fee. Whether an LLC needs an EIN depends on its circumstances and federal tax classification. The IRS is the official source — paying a third party is not necessary to obtain the EIN itself. Step 6: Open a Business Bank Account

Massachusetts vs. Neighboring States: Massachusetts has a $500 formation fee AND $500 annual report. Connecticut, by comparison, charges $120 to form and $80/year for the annual report. A lower filing fee in another state does not necessarily make that state appropriate for a business operating in Massachusetts — foreign qualification requirements may still apply.

Annual Report and Ongoing Compliance

Every Massachusetts LLC must file an annual report with the Secretary of the Commonwealth. The annual report fee is $500, due on the anniversary of the LLC’s formation. This anniversary-based deadline is particularly important — set a recurring reminder well before the deadline. Use our Annual Report Tracker.

The Massachusetts Corporations Division lists a $100 fee for amendments to LLC filings. A Certificate of Good Standing costs $25. Failure to maintain required filings can put an LLC’s active status at risk through administrative dissolution.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Mistake 1 — Looking Only at the $500 Formation Fee: Massachusetts LLCs also have a $500 annual report fee. Build recurring compliance costs into your business budget before forming the LLC.
Mistake 2 — Missing the Anniversary-Based Annual Report: Massachusetts does not use one universal annual-report date for every LLC. Your LLC’s annual report is tied to its formation anniversary. Set a recurring reminder well before the deadline.
Mistake 3 — Assuming Another State Automatically Saves Money: A cheaper formation fee in another state does not necessarily eliminate Massachusetts obligations. If the company is actually doing business in Massachusetts, foreign registration and Massachusetts tax or licensing requirements may still apply.
Mistake 4 — Treating an EIN as a Paid Service: The IRS issues EINs directly at no charge. If a formation company offers EIN filing for an additional charge, remember that you can apply directly through the IRS when eligible.
KEY TAKEAWAY

Starting a Massachusetts LLC begins with a $500 Certificate of Organization and continues with an annual compliance obligation that includes a $500 annual report due on the formation anniversary date. Massachusetts’s personal income tax is 5%, with an additional 4% Fair Share surtax above the applicable threshold. Sales tax is 6.25% statewide. Verify current state fees, filing instructions, and local licensing rules before making a filing decision.

Frequently Asked Questions

1How much does it cost to form an LLC in Massachusetts?
The Massachusetts Certificate of Organization filing fee for a domestic LLC is $500. Other optional or business-specific costs can apply, including name reservation ($30), resident-agent services, licenses, permits, and professional services.
2How much is the Massachusetts LLC annual report?
The Massachusetts LLC annual report fee is $500.
3When is the Massachusetts LLC annual report due?
The annual report is generally due on the anniversary of the LLC’s formation. If your LLC was formed on March 15, its annual report is generally due each year on or before March 15.
4Does Massachusetts require an operating agreement?
Massachusetts does not require an operating agreement to be filed with the state. An operating agreement can nevertheless be useful for documenting ownership, management, voting, distributions, and other internal rules.
5Does Massachusetts require a registered agent?
Massachusetts requires an LLC to maintain a resident agent with an appropriate Massachusetts address for service of process.
6Can I be my own Massachusetts resident agent?
You may be able to serve as your own resident agent if you meet the applicable Massachusetts requirements, including maintaining the required Massachusetts address and availability to receive service of process.
7How much does an EIN cost?
The IRS does not charge a fee to issue an EIN. Eligible businesses can apply directly through the IRS. A third party may charge a fee for completing the application, but paying a third party is not necessary to obtain the EIN itself.
8Does Massachusetts have a state income tax?
Yes. Massachusetts generally applies a 5% personal income tax rate to most taxable income. An additional 4% surtax applies on taxable income above the applicable annual threshold under the state’s Fair Share provisions.
9Does an LLC pay Massachusetts sales tax?
An LLC may need to collect Massachusetts sales tax if it sells taxable goods or services and meets the applicable registration requirements. Massachusetts’s standard sales and use tax rate is 6.25%. Sales-tax obligations depend on the business’s activities.
10Do I need a DBA in Massachusetts?
If your LLC operates under a business name different from its legal name, Massachusetts generally requires a business certificate through the applicable city or town. Requirements and fees for business certificates are handled locally.
11Can I form an LLC in another state instead?
You can generally form a business entity in another state, but if the business actually operates in Massachusetts, it may still need to register as a foreign entity and meet Massachusetts tax, licensing, and compliance requirements.
12How long does it take to form a Massachusetts LLC?
Processing time varies by filing method and current Corporations Division workload. Check the Secretary of the Commonwealth’s current processing information before filing if timing is important.
