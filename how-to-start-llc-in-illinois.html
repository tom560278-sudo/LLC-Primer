How to Start an LLC in Illinois in 2026

Last reviewed: August 22, 2026  ·  Primary sources: Illinois Secretary of State, IDOR, DCEO

To start an LLC in Illinois in 2026, file Articles of Organization with the Illinois Secretary of State, appoint an Illinois registered agent, provide the required business and management information, and pay the $150 state filing fee. Standard online formation is currently listed at about 10 business days; 24-hour service costs $250 total ($150 filing fee + $100 expedite charge).

Illinois also has a few rules that are easy to misunderstand. The $75 Annual Report is due before the first day of the LLC’s anniversary month. An LLC using an assumed name files that assumed name with the Illinois Secretary of State, not automatically with a county clerk. And the state’s 1.5% Personal Property Replacement Tax is not a blanket tax on every LLC — it depends on how the LLC is treated for federal tax purposes.

Can You Form an Illinois LLC Yourself?

You can self-file a straightforward Illinois LLC. The Secretary of State provides an online Articles of Organization process, and a formation company is not legally required. A service can still be useful when it solves a specific need — such as registered-agent coverage, filing support, privacy-related address handling, or compliance reminders. A lawyer may be appropriate when ownership, professional licensing, investor rights, tax structure, or an unusual operating agreement makes the setup more complex.

Choose the Illinois LLC Name — and Understand the DBA Rule

An Illinois LLC name must be distinguishable in the Secretary of State’s records and include Limited Liability Company, LLC, or L.L.C. Before filing, check the state record using the Illinois Secretary of State’s business search. Illinois also allows a name reservation for $25. Reservation is optional; if you are ready to form immediately, you can normally move directly to the Articles of Organization.

Illinois DBA / Assumed Name Rule — Important Difference

⚠ Do not copy the county-clerk rule from a sole proprietorship. Illinois sole proprietors and general partnerships using an assumed name generally work through the local county clerk. An LLC is different: domestic and foreign LLCs in good standing can adopt an assumed name through the Illinois Secretary of State. The 2026 adoption fee is $120 because Illinois bases the fee on where the filing year falls in its five-year assumed-name cycle.

One LLC can adopt assumed names, but the assumed names do not create separate legal entities. Liabilities and obligations still belong to the same underlying LLC. For more, see our LLC operating agreement guide.

Registered Agent vs. Principal Place of Business: Illinois Treats Them Separately

Every Illinois LLC must appoint and maintain a registered agent. The agent may be an Illinois resident or an entity authorized to transact business in Illinois. The registered office must be located in Illinois and must use a qualifying physical address; a PO Box alone is not enough.

The principal place of business is a different field. The Illinois Secretary of State’s domestic LLC guide says the principal place of business may or may not be in Illinois. The filing form requires a physical address where company records are kept; a PO Box alone or a simple “c/o” entry is not acceptable.

ℹ What a Registered Agent Can — and Cannot — Solve: A registered-agent service solves the statutory registered-office requirement and gives the state a reliable place for legal and official mail. It does not automatically convert that office into your genuine principal place of business, bank address, licensing address, or every other address a third party may request. If you plan to use a provider or virtual-office address for the principal-place field, confirm that it satisfies Illinois’s filing requirements before submitting the Articles.

What Goes Into the Illinois Articles of Organization?

Form LLC-5.5 creates the domestic LLC. The form asks for more than a business name and registered agent, so review each field before submitting.

Illinois LLC-5.5 — Core Formation Information
1
LLC legal name
The name must satisfy Illinois naming rules and include an accepted LLC designator.
2
Principal place of business
Physical business/records address. Illinois’s guide says it may be inside or outside Illinois. PO Box alone is not acceptable.
3
Effective date
Normally the filing date, or a later date not more than 60 days after filing.
4
Registered agent and registered office
The registered office must be in Illinois and meet the state’s physical-address requirement.
5
Purpose
A normal LLC can generally state any lawful business purpose unless a regulated activity requires more specific provisions.
6
Managers and authorized members
The Articles must list the names and business addresses of all managers and any member with the authority of manager.

The online filing confirmation is sent after the Department of Business Services examines and approves the filing. Keep the approved Articles with the LLC’s permanent records.

What to Do After Illinois Approves the LLC

Formation creates the state entity; it does not finish the business setup. Most owners should next prepare the operating agreement, obtain an EIN when needed, separate business finances, determine Illinois tax-registration obligations, and identify industry/local licenses before operating.

1

Illinois LLC Taxes Depend on the LLC’s Federal Tax Classification

The 1.5% Personal Property Replacement Tax (PPRT) should not be treated as a tax that every Illinois LLC automatically pays. State filing treatment depends in part on the LLC’s federal tax classification.
Federal TreatmentIllinois TreatmentReplacement Tax
Single-member disregarded LLCIncome generally reported on the owner’s Illinois returnNo separate Illinois LLC income-tax filing
PartnershipFiles IL-10651.5% on Illinois net income
S corporationFiles IL-1120-ST1.5% on Illinois net income
C corporationFiles IL-11207% Illinois income tax + 2.5% replacement tax

Individual Illinois income tax remains 4.95%. For partnerships and S corporations, the owners generally report pass-through income at the owner level while the entity itself is subject to replacement tax.

2026 PTE Tax Update: Illinois continues to allow qualifying partnerships and S corporations to elect the 4.95% Pass-Through Entity Tax. For tax years ending on or after December 31, 2026, Illinois expanded the way partnerships may compute the PTE tax base. This is a tax-planning issue, not a default LLC formation fee. Consult a qualified Illinois tax professional before making any elections.

Illinois Tax Registration, Licenses, and Permits

There is not one universal “Illinois LLC license” that every entity buys simply because it formed an LLC. Licensing and registration depend on what the business does and where it operates.

  • IDOR registration is required when the business has sales tax, sales for resale, certain use/excise taxes, or Illinois employee withholding responsibilities. Register electronically through MyTax Illinois.
  • Professional or industry licensing may come from IDFPR or another state agency.
  • Cities and counties can impose their own business-license, zoning, occupancy, health, or other requirements.
  • Chicago businesses should check Chicago-specific licensing and zoning in addition to state requirements.
🚨 Do not confuse formation with permission to operate: Filing LLC-5.5 creates the legal entity. It does not automatically satisfy tax registration, professional licensing, municipal permits, zoning, or federal regulatory requirements that apply to the business activity.

Illinois LLC Annual Report: $75 and an Unusual Deadline

Every Illinois LLC must file an Annual Report to maintain its existence. The fee is $75, and the deadline is tied to the company’s anniversary month.

🚨 The report is due BEFORE the anniversary month starts: If your Illinois LLC was organized on September 15, the Annual Report is due before September 1 each year — not by September 15 or the end of September. Filing 60 days after the due date triggers a $100 late-filing penalty. If the report remains unfiled within 120 days from delinquency, the LLC can be administratively dissolved.

The Secretary of State typically sends the registered agent a pre-filled report before the due date, but the LLC remains responsible for filing even if a reminder is missed. The Annual Report can update the principal business address and management information; changing the registered agent or registered office uses a separate filing. Should an Illinois-Based Business Form in Wyoming or Another State Instead?

Forming in another state does not automatically remove Illinois obligations. A foreign LLC that must transact business in Illinois uses Form LLC-45.5 to apply for admission, and the current Illinois filing fee is $150. That can leave a genuinely Illinois-based business maintaining one entity in the formation state plus Illinois foreign registration, registered-agent coverage, and recurring compliance. Whether your exact activity requires Illinois admission is a legal and factual question. Consider the actual business operations, registration requirements, costs, and compliance obligations before choosing another state solely for a perceived privacy or tax advantage.
ILLINOIS LLC — 2026 SETUP ORDER
  1. Choose the legal LLC name and confirm availability.
  2. Decide what valid principal-place address you will use and appoint the Illinois registered agent.
  3. File LLC-5.5 and pay the $150 state fee.
  4. Prepare the operating agreement and obtain an EIN when your situation requires one.
  5. Determine IDOR registration and state/local licensing requirements before operating.
  6. If you use another public-facing name, adopt the Illinois LLC assumed name through the Secretary of State.
  7. Calendar the $75 Annual Report for before the first day of the anniversary month.

Frequently Asked Questions

1How much does it cost to start an Illinois LLC?
The Illinois Secretary of State filing fee for standard Articles of Organization is $150. The 24-hour formation option costs $250 total ($150 filing fee + $100 expedite charge). A regular Illinois LLC also files a $75 Annual Report each year before the first day of the LLC's anniversary month.
2How long does Illinois take to approve an LLC online?
Illinois currently lists standard online Articles of Organization at about 10 business days. The 24-hour option costs an additional $100, for a $250 total filing charge. Processing times can change, so verify current estimates with the Illinois Secretary of State before filing.
3Can I be my own registered agent in Illinois?
Yes, if you satisfy Illinois's registered-agent requirements. An individual agent must live in Illinois, and the registered office must be a qualifying physical Illinois address where service of process can be received.
4Does my principal place of business have to be in Illinois?
No. The Illinois Secretary of State's domestic LLC guide says the principal place of business may or may not be in Illinois. The address must still satisfy the filing requirements; a PO Box alone or a simple c/o entry is not acceptable.
5Can I use my registered agent's address as the principal business address?
Do not assume that the registered-agent address is valid for the separate principal-place field. The registered office and principal place of business serve different functions. If you plan to use a provider or virtual-office address for the principal-place field, confirm that it satisfies Illinois's filing requirements before submitting the Articles.
6Where does an Illinois LLC file a DBA?
An Illinois LLC in good standing can adopt an assumed name through the Illinois Secretary of State. The county-clerk assumed-name process generally applies to sole proprietorships and general partnerships, not to the LLC assumed-name filing.
7How much is an Illinois LLC assumed name in 2026?
The Illinois LLC assumed-name adoption fee is $120 for a filing made in 2026. Illinois uses a five-year fee cycle, so the initial adoption fee changes depending on the filing year.
8Does every Illinois LLC pay 1.5% PPRT?
No. The tax treatment depends on federal classification. Partnerships and S corporations are generally subject to 1.5% Personal Property Replacement Tax on Illinois net income. A disregarded single-member LLC reports through its owner and has no separate Illinois LLC income-tax filing requirement. A C corporation uses different rates (7% income tax + 2.5% replacement tax).
9When is the Illinois LLC Annual Report due?
It is due before the first day of the LLC's anniversary month each year. The filing fee is $75. A $100 late-filing penalty applies after 60 days from the due date.
10When can Illinois administratively dissolve an LLC for missing the Annual Report?
The Secretary of State's LLC guide says failure to file within 120 days from delinquency can result in administrative dissolution.
11Can a non-U.S. resident form an Illinois LLC?
Illinois LLC formation does not generally require every member to be a U.S. citizen or Illinois resident. However, the LLC must maintain a qualifying Illinois registered agent and registered office, and non-U.S. owners may have additional federal tax and identification requirements.
