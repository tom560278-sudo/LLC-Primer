What Does a Registered Agent Actually Do?

The Legal Requirement

Every LLC in every US state is legally required to have a Registered Agent. This is the person or company designated to receive official legal and government documents on behalf of your business. Without one, your state won't approve your LLC formation — and if you lose your RA later, the state can dissolve your LLC.

What Documents They Receive

DOCUMENT TYPE WHAT IT IS WHY IT MATTERS
Service of Process Official notice that your LLC is being sued Missing this can result in a default judgment against you
State Correspondence Annual report notices, tax forms, compliance letters Missing deadlines leads to penalties or LLC dissolution. Check your state's deadline →
IRS Notices Tax-related communications from the federal government Ignoring IRS notices can trigger audits or liens
Legal Notices Subpoenas, court orders, regulatory actions These have strict response deadlines — missing them has serious consequences

Can You Be Your Own Registered Agent?

Technically, yes. However, being your own RA means listing your home address on public records, being available at that address during all business hours (typically 9am-5pm), and handling legal documents yourself. For most people, paying $119–$149/yr for a professional service is well worth the convenience and privacy.

3 Registered Agent Mistakes to Avoid

Before you choose a Registered Agent, make sure you don't fall into these common traps. Each one can cost you hundreds of dollars or put your LLC at risk.

1
Paying More Than $150/Year for RA Service

If you're paying $199/yr (ZenBusiness) or $249/yr (LegalZoom) for Registered Agent service, you're overpaying. The service is essentially the same across all providers — someone receives your mail and scans it. Northwest charges $125/yr and Bizee charges $119/yr for the exact same thing. Over 5 years, switching from LegalZoom to Bizee saves you $650. That's money better spent on your actual business — see our full LLC cost breakdown by state to understand the complete picture.

2
Ignoring Privacy Protection

When you form an LLC, your Registered Agent's address goes on state records. If you're your own RA or use a service that doesn't offer privacy, your home address becomes public. Data brokers scrape this within days, and you'll start getting junk mail, spam calls, and your address will appear in Google results. Once it's out there, you can't take it back. Northwest is the only major service that uses their own address on your filings by default — and it's worth choosing them for this reason alone. ⚖️ Business & Industrial

3
Forgetting to Check Auto-Renewal Terms

Some services bundle RA with "compliance packages" that auto-renew at premium prices. For instance, ZenBusiness's Worry-Free Compliance auto-renews at $199/yr, and LegalZoom's attorney plan renews at $49/month. Before signing up, check exactly what auto-renews and set calendar reminders to cancel anything you don't need. Better yet, choose a service like Northwest that has zero auto-renewing subscriptions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a Registered Agent for my LLC? ▼
Yes. Every LLC in every US state is legally required to have a Registered Agent with a physical street address in the state of formation to receive official state notices and legal service of process.
What is the cheapest Registered Agent service? ▼
Bizee offers a free Registered Agent for 1 year when you form your LLC through their $0 plan, with renewals at $119/year. Northwest Registered Agent includes a free 1st year with their $39 LLC formation package, with renewals at $125/year and full privacy protection included.
Can I change my Registered Agent later? ▼
Yes. You can change your Registered Agent at any time by submitting a "Statement of Change of Registered Agent" form with your Secretary of State and paying a small state filing fee (ranging from $0 to $50 depending on the state).
Can I be my own Registered Agent? ▼
Yes, you can act as your own Registered Agent in all 50 states as long as you are at least 18 years old, reside in the state where your LLC is formed, and maintain a physical address (not a P.O. Box) where you are available during regular business hours. However, doing so makes your address a public record.
Which Registered Agent offers the best privacy? ▼
Northwest Registered Agent is the best choice for privacy. They automatically use their own office address on your public state filings in all 50 states, keeping your personal home address off state databases and search engines.
Is it worth paying more for a premium Registered Agent? ▼
No. Services charging $199 to $249 per year (like ZenBusiness or LegalZoom) deliver the same core mail scanning and forwarding as $119–$125 services like Bizee or Northwest. Unless you specifically require built-in attorney consultations, paying over $150/year is unnecessary.
