What Does a Registered Agent Actually Do?

If you're new to LLCs, the term "Registered Agent" might sound intimidating. In reality, it's a simple but legally required role — you can read our full Registered Agent guide for the complete breakdown, or keep reading for the quick version. 🏭 Business & Industrial

The Legal Requirement

Every LLC in every US state is legally required to have a Registered Agent. This is the person or company designated to receive official legal and government documents on behalf of your business. Without one, your state will reject your Articles of Organization or revoke your LLC's good standing.

Your Registered Agent must have a physical street address in your state of formation (P.O. Boxes are not allowed) and be available during regular business hours (9am to 5pm, Monday through Friday) to receive service of process (lawsuits, subpoenas, state notices).

3 Registered Agent Mistakes to Avoid

Avoid these common pitfalls when choosing or serving as your own Registered Agent:

  • 1. Listing Your Home Address as Your Registered Agent Address: When you serve as your own Registered Agent, your physical address becomes a public record. It can be viewed by anyone on the Secretary of State website, scraped by junk mailers, and indexed by Google. Using a professional Registered Agent service like Northwest keeps your personal home address 100% private.
  • 2. Missing State Compliance Notices or Lawsuits: If you serve as your own agent and are away from home, traveling, or miss a process server delivering court documents, you risk receiving a default judgment against your business. Professional services scan legal mail on the day it arrives and alert you instantly.
  • 3. Overpaying for Standard RA Renewal: Many providers offer a "free" first year but charge $200–$250/yr upon renewal. Over 5 years, paying $249/yr costs $1,245 compared to $500 with Northwest or $476 with Bizee. Always check the year-2 renewal rate before selecting a provider.

Frequently Asked Questions

01. Can I be my own Registered Agent for my LLC? ▼
Yes, you can act as your own Registered Agent in all 50 states as long as you are at least 18 years old, reside in the state where your LLC is formed, and maintain a physical address (not a P.O. Box) where you are available during regular business hours. However, doing so makes your address a public record.
02. What is the cheapest Registered Agent service? ▼
Bizee offers a free Registered Agent for 1 year when you form your LLC through their $0 plan, with renewals at $119/year. Northwest Registered Agent includes a free 1st year with their $39 LLC formation package, with renewals at $125/year and full privacy protection included.
03. Does a Registered Agent address hide my personal address? ▼
Only if the Registered Agent provider allows you to use their office address on public Secretary of State filings. Northwest Registered Agent explicitly permits using their address on all state documents, keeping your home address off public records. Competitors like Bizee, ZenBusiness, and LegalZoom still require your own address on certain filing fields unless you purchase separate virtual address packages.
04. Can I change my Registered Agent later? ▼
Yes. You can change your Registered Agent at any time by submitting a "Statement of Change of Registered Agent" form with your Secretary of State and paying a small state filing fee (ranging from $0 to $50 depending on the state).
05. Do I need a Registered Agent in every state if I expand? ▼
Yes. If your LLC registers to do business as a Foreign LLC in another state, you must designate a Registered Agent with a physical address in that state. Northwest offers a discounted multi-state rate of $100/yr per state for 5 or more states.
