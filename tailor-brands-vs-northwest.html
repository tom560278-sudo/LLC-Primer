Frequently Asked Questions

01 Is Northwest better than Tailor Brands for LLC formation?
Neither service is universally better. Northwest is more focused on formation and registered-agent services, while Tailor Brands combines formation with branding and business-building tools.
02 Does Tailor Brands include registered agent service?
No. Tailor Brands currently lists registered-agent service separately at $199/year. It is not included in the advertised Lite, Essential or Elite formation-plan price.
03 How much does Northwest Registered Agent cost?
Northwest currently advertises LLC formation at $39 + state fees. The formation package includes the first year of registered-agent service and an EIN. After the included registered-agent period, the current renewal price is $125/year.
04 Does Northwest include an EIN?
Yes. Northwest currently includes EIN service with its LLC formation package. However, eligible businesses can also obtain an EIN directly from the IRS for free.
05 Does Tailor Brands include an EIN?
Tailor Brands offers EIN-related services separately. An EIN itself is free when obtained directly from the IRS by an eligible applicant.
06 Does Tailor Brands include an operating agreement?
The current Tailor Brands pricing page lists an operating agreement as included with Essential and Elite. It is not listed as an included Lite feature.
07 Does Northwest include an operating agreement?
Northwest includes an operating agreement template with its formation service. A template is not the same thing as individualized legal advice.
08 Which company has better privacy protection?
Northwest places greater emphasis on privacy within its registered-agent service, using its address for RA records where legally permitted. However, this does not mean that every personal address is hidden — state formation documents can require additional address information.
09 Can Tailor Brands build my website?
Yes. Tailor Brands' Elite plan includes website-building tools, a one-year domain, and other branding features.
10 Does Northwest provide logo or website tools?
Branding and website creation are not the primary focus of Northwest's LLC formation service. If you want an integrated logo and website platform, Tailor Brands has more relevant features.
11 Which service is better for a first LLC?
For straightforward formation and registered-agent support, Northwest is more focused on those tasks. For formation combined with branding and website creation, Tailor Brands may be more appropriate.
