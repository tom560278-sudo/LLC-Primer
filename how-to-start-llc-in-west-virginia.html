How to Start an LLC in West Virginia in 2026

Last reviewed: October 2026  ·  Primary sources: West Virginia Secretary of State, West Virginia State Tax Division

West Virginia charges $100 to form an LLC with the Secretary of State. New businesses also generally need a West Virginia Business Registration Certificate, which costs $30 for most businesses. Online Secretary of State filings have an additional $1 processing fee.

West Virginia LLCs must also file a state report after formation. The standard fee is $25 per year, although qualifying businesses can elect biennial reporting after meeting the state’s five-year timely-filing requirement. A biennial report costs $50. Reports are due by June 30.

West Virginia’s 2026 individual income-tax rates range from 2.11% to 4.58% after legislation enacted in 2026 reduced the rates by 5% retroactive to January 1, 2026.

Step 1: Name Your West Virginia LLC

Your West Virginia LLC name must be distinguishable from names already registered or reserved with the Secretary of State. West Virginia does offer name reservation — you can reserve an available business name for 120 days for $15. Name reservation is optional; you can also proceed directly to formation if you are ready to file. Your legal name must use an appropriate LLC designation such as “Limited Liability Company,” “Limited Company,” “LLC,” “L.L.C.,” “LC,” or “L.C.”

Step 2: Agent for Service of Process

West Virginia law permits an LLC to maintain an agent and address for service of process, but the statute does not make appointment of an agent mandatory for every LLC. The Articles of Organization specifically allow the initial agent information to be provided “if any.” If you do appoint an agent for service of process, the agent must meet West Virginia’s eligibility requirements and have an appropriate West Virginia address.

Step 3: File Articles of Organization

The Articles of Organization create your West Virginia LLC. You file them with the West Virginia Secretary of State and pay the $100 formation fee. Online filings have an additional $1 processing fee. West Virginia encourages businesses to use the One Stop Business Portal, which can coordinate registration with the Secretary of State and other state agencies.

Fee Waivers: West Virginia provides an initial Secretary of State registration-fee waiver for qualifying veteran-owned businesses and West Virginia-resident young entrepreneurs ages 18–29. These programs waive the initial Secretary of State business registration fee; they should not be treated as a blanket waiver of every state or tax-related fee.

Step 4: West Virginia Business Registration Certificate

If your LLC is engaged in business activity that requires registration, you must obtain a West Virginia Business Registration Certificate before beginning business activity. The certificate is issued by the West Virginia State Tax Division, not the Secretary of State. The standard fee is $30, although certain businesses qualify for an exemption from the fee.

Small Business Exception: West Virginia’s tax guidance includes an exception for certain businesses with $4,000 or less in gross business income from operations in all states during the most recently completed income-tax year, provided the other statutory conditions are satisfied. Do not assume every small LLC automatically qualifies — review the State Tax Division’s current requirements.

Step 5: Create an Operating Agreement

West Virginia does not require every LLC to file an operating agreement with the Secretary of State. An operating agreement can document ownership percentages, member contributions, profit and loss allocations, voting rights, management responsibilities, procedures for adding or removing members, what happens if a member leaves, and dissolution procedures. Banks, lenders, investors, and other institutions may request formation documents or an operating agreement when verifying who owns or controls the company.

Step 6: Get Your EIN

An Employer Identification Number (EIN) is the federal tax identification number issued by the IRS. The IRS issues EINs free of charge. An EIN is commonly needed when an LLC has employees, has multiple members, elects certain federal tax classifications, opens a business bank account, or needs a federal tax identification number for business purposes. Apply directly through the IRS rather than paying a third party simply to obtain an EIN.

Step 7: Open a Business Bank Account

A dedicated business bank account helps keep LLC finances separate from personal finances. Separating business and personal transactions also makes bookkeeping, tax reporting, and financial recordkeeping easier. Typical documents a bank may request: Articles of Organization, EIN, operating agreement, Business Registration Certificate, and government-issued identification. Bank requirements vary, so confirm the institution’s current documentation requirements before opening the account.

West Virginia LLC Taxes

West Virginia LLCs are generally treated as pass-through businesses for federal income-tax purposes by default, although the exact tax treatment depends on the LLC’s ownership and federal tax classification. Understanding how LLCs are taxed at the federal level is the starting point before evaluating West Virginia-specific obligations.

Annual and Biennial Reporting

West Virginia changed its reporting rules effective July 1, 2026. The standard requirement remains an annual report costing $25. However, an LLC that has timely filed required annual reports for five consecutive calendar years and is in good standing can elect to move to biennial reporting. A biennial report costs $50 and is filed every two years.

Who Can Elect Biennial Reporting? An LLC can elect biennial reporting after it has: (1) timely filed all required annual reports for five consecutive calendar years; and (2) remained in good standing with the Secretary of State. If an LLC later fails to timely file its biennial report or loses good standing, the Secretary of State can revoke the biennial option — after which the business must return to annual reporting for five consecutive calendar years before becoming eligible again.
Veteran-Owned Businesses: West Virginia provides an annual-report fee waiver for qualifying veteran-owned registered entities for the first four years after initial formation. The report itself still has to be filed.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Mistake 1: Treating the Business Registration Certificate as Optional
If your business activity requires registration, you must obtain the Business Registration Certificate before commencing business activity. Operating without the required certificate can result in penalties.
Mistake 2: Using July 1 as the Annual Report Deadline
The current statutory deadline is June 30 at 11:59 PM. Do not use the old July 1 wording for 2026.
Mistake 3: Assuming the LLC Can Immediately Use Biennial Reporting
Biennial reporting is not automatically available to every newly formed LLC. The business generally must first have five consecutive years of timely annual filings and good standing before making the election.
Mistake 4: Treating the $30 Business Registration Fee as Universal
The standard fee is $30, but West Virginia lists exemptions for certain businesses. Check the State Tax Division’s current requirements rather than assuming every business owes the fee.
Mistake 5: Assuming a Registered Agent Is Mandatory
West Virginia’s LLC statute permits an agent for service of process but does not impose the same universal agent requirement found in many other states. If you choose an agent, make sure the agent meets West Virginia’s statutory requirements.
West Virginia LLC — Key Takeaway

A typical West Virginia LLC starts with a $100 Secretary of State formation fee and, for businesses required to register, a $30 Business Registration Certificate fee. The annual report is $25 due by June 30 each year, with a $50 biennial option available after five consecutive years of timely annual filings and good standing. West Virginia’s 2026 individual income-tax rates range from 2.11% to 4.58%, while the state sales-tax rate is 6%.

Frequently Asked Questions

1How much does it cost to start an LLC in West Virginia?
The standard Secretary of State formation fee is $100. Businesses required to obtain a Business Registration Certificate generally pay an additional $30. Online Secretary of State filings also have a $1 processing fee. Optional expenses include name reservation ($15), registered-agent services, formation services, licenses, and permits.
2When is the West Virginia LLC annual report due?
The report is due by 11:59 PM on June 30. The same June 30 deadline applies to an applicable biennial report.
3Does West Virginia offer biennial LLC reports?
Yes. An LLC can elect biennial reporting after it has timely filed all required annual reports for five consecutive calendar years and is in good standing. The biennial report fee is $50.
4Does West Virginia require a registered agent for an LLC?
West Virginia law allows an LLC to maintain an agent and address for service of process but does not require every LLC to appoint one. The Articles of Organization provide for agent information “if any.”
5Does West Virginia require a Business Registration Certificate?
Businesses engaging in business activity that is subject to West Virginia’s registration requirements generally must obtain a Business Registration Certificate before beginning operations. Certain statutory exceptions apply.
6How much does the Business Registration Certificate cost?
The standard Business Registration Certificate fee is $30. West Virginia lists several categories that may be exempt from paying the registration fee, so verify your specific situation with the State Tax Division.
7What are West Virginia’s 2026 individual income-tax rates?
For most individual taxpayers, the 2026 progressive rates range from 2.11% to 4.58%. The rates were reduced by legislation enacted in 2026 and apply retroactively to January 1, 2026: 2.11% on $0–$10,000; 2.81% on $10,001–$25,000; 3.16% on $25,001–$40,000; 4.22% on $40,001–$60,000; 4.58% on over $60,000.
8Can veterans receive West Virginia LLC fee waivers?
Qualifying veteran-owned businesses can receive a waiver of the initial Secretary of State business-registration fee. West Virginia also provides an annual-report fee waiver for qualifying veteran-owned registered entities for the first four years after formation. These programs have specific eligibility requirements.
9What is West Virginia’s sales tax rate?
West Virginia’s state sales tax rate is 6%. Businesses with taxable sales may have additional registration, collection, filing, and remittance obligations.
